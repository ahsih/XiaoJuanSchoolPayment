## Decisions

- The page moved onto the shared Philippine template in CIA's compact layout, with single, group and 方案对比 quotes and `layoutVersion: 2` images.
- 思达启航折扣: adult ESL/ESL2 course and room ×0.9 (resort fee and registration excluded). Peak Family Camp: 25 dollars per person per peak week (the school's “100美金/4周/每人”). Off-season family courses (家长上课 and Family Support): no discount, per the school's answer. Returning students: 100 dollars per person off registration.
- Adult ESL/ESL2 run only off-season and only for adults; a quote touching a peak week or with a minor is blocked.
- Off-season family stays run 4–8 weeks, any length (user answer 2026-11-24), priced at the four-week price ÷ 4 × weeks; 1–3 weeks only in the peak Family Camp. Peak Family Camp allows 1–8 weeks (45/65/85% for 1–3). A stay crossing peak and off-season prices each part by its own season, as the school's example (peak ÷ 4 × peak weeks + off ÷ 4 × off weeks); SSP, SSP I-Card and pickup are paid once.
- Family quotes are whole-family, single quote only; a group quote containing a family course is blocked. Members studying different weeks go through the adviser.
- Local fees: the 2025 sheet for first arrivals in 2026, the 2027 sheet from 2027-01-01. Upkeep (管理费 / Utilities) and electricity-water are charged per started four weeks; the school settles the final amount. Peak-only family stays show the local fees as included.
- Visa extensions default to a 59-day entry; the 30-day entry is selectable. Rates 5,130 / 6,400 / 4,430 pesos (30-day entry), the 59-day entry starting at 6,400.
- 2026 Christmas offer (100 dollars, study touching 2026/12/20–2027/01/02, at least 4 weeks): adults and every family member, 100 dollars per person (user answers 2026-11-24).
- ESL2 night classes: the orientation guide says there are none on Thursday (mock test) and Friday (graduation); the user's note said “周四除外”. The page follows the orientation guide.
- The 5–8-person room photos come from the Drive folder “海滨5-8人间”; they are shown in the gallery only, not tied to a price card. Their caption says 2-person and larger families can be placed there (user answer 2026-11-24).
- Speaking Master: the 2026 H2 poster and one slide of the Encanto presentation name it, but Encanto's own ESL price chart and course flyer list only ESL and ESL2, so it is not on the page.
- Videos: the eight clips are 720×1280 H.264 with poster frames in `public/assets/smeag-encanto/videos/`, grouped as 校园／课程／宿舍. The sea-view room's bedding matches the room in the school's campus tour. The family activity clip is left out because a child's T-shirt shows the flag of the Taiwan authorities and the word TAIWAN.
- Never published: the school's address, website, phone, QR codes, Wi-Fi names and passwords, bank details, agent commission, and the poster showing a Taiwan flag.
