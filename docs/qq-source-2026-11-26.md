## Prices as built (all in dollars unless pesos)

- Courses by class days (Mon–Fri less closed days): 1–14 days fixed totals, then a day rate for 15–19 / 20–39 / 40+ days. 4 1:1 39.2/39.2/38; 4+2 44.2/44.2/42.82; 6 1:1 55.38/53.88/53.88; 6+2 60.38/58.88/58.7; 8 1:1 73.84/71.84/71.84. Four weeks: 784 / 884 / 1,077.6 / 1,177.6 / 1,436.8.
- Closed days (no class, not charged): 2026/12/24, 12/25, 12/31; 2027/01/01, 03/25, 03/26, 11/01, 12/24, 12/25, 12/31 (both campuses; ITP Sinulog 2027/01/17 is a Sunday).
- Holiday Fee 35 a day for holidays with class: ITP 2026/12/08, 12/30, 2027/02/24, 04/09, 08/06, 08/30, 09/09, 11/02, 11/30, 12/08, 12/30; BFC the same with 04/27 instead of 02/24. These days are also charged as class days (school system).
- Rooms per night, Sunday to the leaving Saturday (4 weeks = 27 nights): capsule 17.15 (under 27 nights) / 13, per person, 15+ only; Treewoods 50 a room; Savoy as above, per room.
- Meals: 1 week 105; otherwise 10 a calendar day from the first to the last class day (4 weeks 260).
- Registration 150 (returning students who studied after 2022/07/01: 0); pickup 30 a person.
- Local fees (pesos): SSP 12,000; visa extension by days in the Philippines (30-day visa 31–59: 7,230, 60–89: 9,210, 90–119: 13,250, 120–149: 15,230, 150–179: 19,270; 59-day visa 60–89: 7,230 …); I-Card 4,000 over 59 days; student ID 420; electricity 110 a capsule night; textbooks about 1,000–2,000, outside the total.

## Open questions

1. BESA/CALA 2027 peak 60 dollars/week (7/4–8/28) for QQ English (quote-rules.md) vs the school: "暂时没有暑假附加费" and no surcharge in its system. User decision 2026-11-26: follow the school, no surcharge.
2. Is the 35-dollar Holiday Fee compulsory for every holiday with class? The system adds it automatically; the price sheet calls it 假期费 / 假期增课.
3. Savoy photos: which room type each shows (standard or junior suite) is not stated; the page labels them only as Savoy rooms.
4. Campus photos (classrooms, capsule, canteen) are in the school's WeChat album mini program; automated clicks do not reach it, so the user is to save them.
5. IELTS (BFC Regular/Sparta), ITP KET and camps are not on the pages.
6. Two-campus stays: the school bills each campus separately; the pages quote one campus each.
