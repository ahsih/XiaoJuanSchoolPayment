## Facts taken

**School:** Emerald Cultural Institute (爱蒙路学院 in the leaflet), slogan "Experience Tradition, Embrace Innovation". Students from more than 60 countries. Lessons are 60 minutes. Online oral and written placement test. Teachers available in the afternoon; free afternoon Study Club; staff-led afternoon social and cultural activities; free workshops and talks (living & working in Ireland, CV & interview, future study options, safety and legal advice, renting in Dublin, cultural norms); help with immigration/IRP and PPS number; free GELnet online platform (Oxford University Press content with AI), from 4 weeks before arrival to 3 months after the course; personal tutor meetings with feedback in an app; weekly tests and attendance monitoring; welcome pack (map, student handbook); certificate and student reports; regular progress reports; switching from Intensive to IELTS preparation at no extra cost; exam centre for Cambridge, TIE, TOEIC and Oxford tests; preparation centre for OET, Cambridge and IELTS; work placement and university pathway courses. No beginner courses (p. 7 footnote).

**TrustEd Ireland:** first list published by QQI in August 2026 includes Emerald (school's own deck). TrustEd replaces ILEP (expected end of 2026) as the basis for non-EU study visas over 90 days; schools contribute to the Learner Protection Fund; courses on the TrustEd register are used by ISD for visa/permission decisions. Verify the policy dates on QQI's site before stating them as fact.

**Campus (Palmerston Park, Rathgar, Dublin):** Victorian mansion, landscaped private garden, 6 Luas stops to the city centre, 22 modern classrooms with interactive technology, Wi-Fi across campus, canteen with hot food and café, reading room and study spaces, upmarket residential area, easy access to Dundrum Shopping Centre. (Never publish the street address, Eircode, phone, fax, email or website printed on the back page.)

**Accommodation:** homestay (adults: breakfast and dinner; chosen families; convenient to school; regularly inspected with feedback collected); student residence (apartment style, single room with private bathroom, shared kitchen and lounge). Leaflet says "学生可保证住宿" – do not publish as a promise (rule: only the booked type is promised).
