# Cross-school quote rules

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-11-26 to keep that file small. Record new decisions about this topic here; AGENTS.md holds only rules that apply to every task.

### ACR I-Card with the first visa extension (2026-11-24)

User rule for every Philippine school: ACR I-Card (or the school's I-CARD/VISA I-CARD row) is charged once together with the first tourist-visa extension: from week 5 on a 30-day entry visa and from week 9 on a 59-day entry visa. Each school keeps its own ACR amount and extension prices. Schools whose calculators charged it later were moved on 2026-11-24: B'Cebu, BTES, TARGET, IMS, IU, ICL, EV La Mer, CELLA Uni/Premium, SMEAG Capital, CIEC, HANA, CPILS and English MINT; Cebu Blue Ocean's old calculator charged it to every student, and its template quote (`cboa-page-quote.ts`, 2026-11-24) charges it only with the first extension. `pages/philippines/acr-first-extension.ts` (`upgradeAcrNotes`, `ACR_NOTE_VERSION`, stored as `quoteSettings.acrNoteVersion`) replaces untouched saved ACR notes once, on the fee row and in the quote image, and keeps edited ones; La Mer inlines the same upgrade because its seed generator cannot load imports. Never put `acrNoteVersion` into a defaults object that a clone spreads under saved settings (CELLA, IU/ICL, La Mer, BTES/TARGET, MINT), or old documents would skip the upgrade. CIA is locked and unchanged. Already on the rule: EV, PINES, JIC, HELP, A&J, BECI, CG Banilad/Sparta, CPI, CIP, GLC, I.BREEZE, MONOL, Philinter, WALES and English Fella (since its 2026-11-25 template pages; its former calculator charged the first extension and ACR at 4 weeks on a 30-day visa, the old 27,940-peso 4-week total). Not changed, because their own confirmed rules differ: MONOL Sparta keeps its SSP ACR I-CARD for everyone (the SSP card) and its TVV ACR reference row outside the total (the handbook says immigration does not charge it at present), and CPILS keeps ARP at the second extension. Older school pages without a calculator (CWA, QQ English, Winning and other directory-style pages) only show the school's own wording in reference tables and charge nothing. CELLA and CPILS are bundled by the mini program, so their quotes there change only after `npm run quote:sync` and a release.

### User-confirmed returning and tourist-visa rules (2026-09-20)

The user confirmed that returning students at all Philippine schools waive registration. A 30-day tourist visa requires extension and one ACR I-Card after four study weeks; a 59-day visa requires both after eight weeks. These instructions supersede contradictory historical school notes below. ### Confirmed internal quote rules (2026-09-18)

These user-confirmed rules supersede older ordinary-quote duration/row rules below. All completed school calculators judge the price tier from each student's own total **course** weeks. From four weeks, price every ordinary course/room segment using that type's four-week price divided by four times segment weeks. Under four weeks, retain the campus's confirmed short-stay ratios; never combine different students or course plus room weeks. Schools without confirmed short-stay data block those short quotes rather than invent prices.

`SchoolQuotePlan` now enables continuous, bidirectional stay synchronization by default. Editing either side's first Sunday, weeks, or adding/removing segments aligns both lists' coverage while preserving earlier selections and adjusting the other list's last segment. Segment counts may differ. Every segment can use whole weeks, including 1/2/3 weeks within a longer quote. All model validation rejects non-Sundays, invalid dates, overlaps, gaps and unequal coverage; end dates are start + weeks × 7 − 1 day. GLC family packages are one whole-family quote on one shared plan. TARGET keeps combined course/room package segments.

Keep these algorithms internal: do not add formula, short-stay algorithm, per-student tier or synchronization explanations to public quote forms, image previews, generated images or footers. Preserve necessary existing payment/promotion/age/course requirements. `shortStayNotes` deliberately returns no public text. Published legacy CG Sparta per-row copy and JIC image formula defaults are removed on configuration load without overwriting unrelated employee copy.

Preserve confirmed whole packages, guarantees, free-week offers and shared-room prices. A&J, IMS and BTES use their own price adapters for these structures. CELLA family/social offers, MINT fixed/family packages and BTES ALL IN ONE remain intact; unsupported mixes block export. IU/ICL merge identical adjacent selections for package eligibility, and block unmatched eligible low-season mixed plans with a note to ask the adviser. MINT ordinary segments may differ, but its complete local-fee tables still cover only 4/8/12/16/20/24 total weeks. Do not invent intermediate local fees or PTE/guarantee course prices.

Audit inventory, special cases and verification are in `docs/quote-rules-audit-2026-09-18.md`. Focused cross-school tests: `npx ng test --watch=false --browsers=ChromeHeadlessNoSandbox --ts-config=tsconfig.quote-rules.spec.json --include=src/app/pages/philippines/school-quote-rules.spec.ts --progress=false`. Existing school regressions and published-content/special-package tests use `tsconfig.quote-regression.spec.json` and `tsconfig.quote-special-cases.spec.json` with their matching `--include` paths.

## BESA / CALA 2027 peak surcharge (2026-10-26)

The joint BESA/CALA notice changes the ordinary peak-season surcharge for member academies from 40 dollars per student/week through 2026 to 60 dollars per student/week from 2027. `association-peak-surcharge.ts` is the shared year-sensitive rate authority for completed member-school calculators. It de-duplicates each student's course and accommodation weeks, and the surcharge remains outside registration waivers, fixed promotions and percentage discounts. Employee-published content may define confirmed date ranges but cannot restore the old 40-dollar 2027 rate.

Only school-confirmed or user-confirmed 2027 ranges are enabled: Cebu Blue Ocean 2027/06/27–08/21, PINES 2027/06/27–08/21, CELLA Uni/Premium 2027/07/04–08/28, EV main campus 2027/07/04–08/28, JIC Challenger/Premium 2027/06/27–08/22 (user 2026-11-23) and CG Banilad/Sparta 2027/07/04–08/28 (CALA notice in CG's 2026-11 pack). A&J uses 2027/06/27–08/22 (school notice, 2026-11-24); BECI legacy estimated 2027 ranges are disabled during published-content cloning (Philinter's 2027/07/04–08/28 range is enabled since its 2026-11-24 pack); MONOL, WALES and BTES also remain inactive for 2027 until school-specific dates arrive. CWA, QQ English and Winning have no complete peak-surcharge quote/image integration and are audit-only rather than newly invented. EV La Mer retains its independent ordinary/family-package season pricing. CPILS keeps its own 40-dollar rate for its 2027/07/04–08/28 study weeks (school reply, user decision 2026-11-24) and is not in the member list. CIA, I.BREEZE, B'Cebu and every other non-member rule are unchanged. Mapping, dates and compatibility details are in `docs/association-peak-surcharge-2027-audit.md`.

## Unified quote exchange rates (2026-10-14)

User restored the existing free Frankfurter/ECB source; do not use Baidu, paid API keys, or fixed currency fallbacks. ExchangeRateService fetches the latest CNY-based daily rates directly from the existing v1 endpoint, with all supported ECB currencies and no backend configuration. connectQuoteRates owns each public/employee quote's USD/CNY and PHP/CNY updates, actual observation date, validity flags and component teardown. Both webpage and image use the same rates. Valid recent business-day observations (up to seven calendar days) are allowed, including weekends; never relabel them as today's tick/live intraday rate. Refresh hourly, cache genuine validated responses for at most six hours, and reject malformed, future, older-than-seven-day or non-positive/non-finite rates. With no usable received/cached data, show conversion pending and block image export. All prior fixed PHP/CNY rules (IMS/MINT 8; A&J/MONOL/BTES 9) are superseded. No school pricing/discount rules, seeds or published revisions are changed by this conversion layer. The pure parsing/validation rules live in `services/exchange-rate-rules.ts` (re-exported by the service) and are shared with the WeChat mini program. Source behavior, inventory and validation are in docs/quote-exchange-rates-2026-10-14.md. Focused tests use tsconfig.exchange-rate.spec.json.
