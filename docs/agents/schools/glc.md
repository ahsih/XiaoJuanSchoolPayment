# School notes: glc

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-11-26 to keep that file small. Record new decisions about this topic here; AGENTS.md holds only rules that apply to every task.

GLC on the template (2026-11-25, from the school pack `GLC宣传手册.zip`: 2027 price list, 2027 local-fee sheet, Chinese brochure, orientation, dorm rules, refund rules, 2027 holiday notice and photos). `/philippines-study/cebu/global-language-cebu` renders `GlcSchoolPageComponent` on the shared Philippine template in CIA's compact layout with `layoutVersion: 2` quote and comparison images; `glc-page-content.ts` holds the page text and data, `glc-page-profile.ts` the profile and `glc-page-quote.ts` the student calculation (`GlcPageStudent`, `GlcQuote`, `GlcPlanComparison`). The old `glc-school.component.*`, `glc-pricing.ts`, `glc-quote.ts` and `glc-quote-plan.ts` are gone. `glc-content-config.ts` (code `GLC`) is the content document: 24 courses and 9 rooms (main building, Annex2 and a partner hotel) stored as 4-week prices (the school quotes per week; weekly × 4), one price list for 2026 and 2027 (the clone copies `tuition` into `tuition2027`). Prices come only from it (`applyPricingData` is a no-op; the database still holds the old weekly rows and `SeedGlcPricingAsync` stays the insert-only baseline). `GLC_POLICY_VERSION` 1 replaces an older saved weekly catalogue, local fees, offers and table copy once, keeping on/off switches, media and edited image lines. Every week costs the same (4-week price ÷ 4 × weeks); stays of 1–3 weeks add a 60-dollar weekly short-term charge, shown through the template's optional `shortStayRows` instead of the ratio line. Registration 120 dollars per person, returning students free. Airport pickup is paid with the school fees (Sunday 35, round trip 70; other days 55 / 110 dollars). Offers: the school's annual offer, 150 dollars per four consecutive course weeks wholly inside 2026/06/01–2027/01/02, 2027/02/28–07/03 or 2027/08/29–2028/01/01, for every adult course except Light Power Speaking; 思达启航 50 dollars per four consecutive course weeks on every course; one free Sunday one-way pickup for registrations 2026/04/05–2027/01/02 with at least 4 course weeks, or with the school offer. The 2027 winter (01/03–02/27) and summer (07/04–08/28, at least 4 weeks) take only adults 18+ and a few families, with no offers in those weeks and no surcharge (`peakSeasonsWithoutSurcharge`). Family packages (2/3/4 people) are one whole-family course price; room, registration and local fees are per person, and the family shares a twin room, the main triple or the main quad. Families are quoted one at a time (group mode is blocked) and are left out of 方案对比. Kids English is for 5–11 with a parent; Junior Power Speaking for 12–14 (an unaccompanied junior pays a 150-dollar weekly guardian fee, stays in the main triple and needs a round-trip pickup); 15–17 may take adult courses except Business (18+). Sparta courses live only in the Annex2 single or twin. Local fees (2027 sheet): SSP 8,000, E-Card 4,500, visa extensions 4,670 / 5,740 / 3,970 (a 59-day entry starts at 5,740), ACR I-Card 4,000 with the first extension, management 6,000 per 4 room weeks (1–3 weeks 3,000 / 4,000 / 5,000), dorm electricity 500 a week (none at the partner hotel), ESL online books 3,000 / 5,000 / 7,000 for up to 8 / 16 / 24 weeks, IELTS, TOEIC and Business books per type 5,000 / 7,500 / 10,000 / 15,000; the 3,000-peso deposit is a reference row outside the total. The refund section shows the 思达启航 deposit rows and the school's post-entry rules; pre-entry penalties, the agency payment deadline and commission stay off the page. Photos are in `public/assets/glc/{campus,study,life,rooms}/`; the partner hotel has no photos. Videos (`GLC_VIDEOS` in `glc-page-content.ts`, `public/assets/glc/videos/`, 720p H.264 with posters) are 14 clips from GLC's official YouTube channel (2026 uploads, user request 2026-11-25) and 6 of the school's own social-media clips from the Google Drive folder it shared (2026-11-26: 2026 campus tour, face-ID entry, meals, October 2026 activities, a 2024 weekend-trips reel, a 2026 kids' art workshop; 30 fps), grouped in the hero album as 校园／宿舍／课程／生活／访谈; the main single-room clip opens that room's album. The FPV drone flight is cut to 3:25 (0:00–2:20 and 3:33–4:38) to keep the file small; the Annex2 tour starts in the Annex2 courtyard because the walk there passes another company's branded vans; the young Chinese student's interview leaves out his answer about friends, whose subtitle lists classmates by nationality; a Vietnamese student's interview (Vietnamese flags, classmates listed by nationality) and a sign-in-only clip were not used.
