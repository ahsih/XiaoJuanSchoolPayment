Prices: Ayala from the 2026-09-22 table (valid from 2026-10-03), Ocean from the 2026-06-03 table; no 2027 list yet. Course + room (with weekday meals) per four weeks, 1/2/3 weeks 40/60/80%, longer pro rata; registration 100, returning students free. Ayala rooms: CQH on campus (2P, Family 3P, BP4, BP6; no single rooms on campus), Hotel George off campus (deluxe/standard single, family 2/3/4P), Lyf Hotel off campus (1P, family 2–6P, at least 2 weeks, transport own in off-season). Ocean rooms: 1P, 2P, Family 3P (two adults + one child), female BP4, male BP8. Exam and business courses only at Ayala. Offers: Ayala grand opening (registration by 2026-12-31, off-peak weeks only; peak = 2027/01/24–02/28 and 2027/07/04–08/28): free upgrade to the next level for ESL/IELTS rows of 4+ weeks, then 85%; then 思达启航 5% (the school's CALA agency 95%) at both campuses. Peak surcharge 60 dollars a week 2027/07/04–08/28 (CALA), outside the discounts. Ocean refuses arrivals in Nov–Dec 2026 (school pause). Local fees (pesos): SSP 7,800, Student E-Card 4,500, ACR 4,500 with the first extension, visa extensions 5,140 / 6,920 / 4,440 / 4,950 / 5,450 plus 300 appointment each (59-day visa starts at 6,920), water and power 900 and management 500 per room week, material fee by the school's weeks table, ID 200; deposit 3,500, pickup 1,000 and laundry 200 are outside the total. Children's courses (JEP kids/junior, preschool) need the minor age group; plan comparison leaves out the family courses.
