## BECI campus quote rules

Each student's age,30/59-day tourist visa and optional Manila/Clark pickup are independent. One/two/three-week course and accommodation prices are40%/60%/80% of the four-week rate. All Sida students receive a100-dollar registration discount. Entry on2026/02/08–06/14 or2026/09/06–12/27 makes the full course and accommodation amount90%, then the8/12/16/20/24-week50/100/200/300/400-dollar long-stay discount is subtracted per student. The former 2027/06/27–08/21 estimate is disabled; the confirmed 2027 range will use60 dollars per week and remain outside discounts. All campuses share local fees:7,800-peso SSP,4,500-peso SSP-E Card,4,000-peso ACR on the first required extension,4,940 pesos per additional30 days,1,000-peso maintenance,3,000-peso water/electricity and1,500-peso laundry per started four accommodation weeks,2,000-peso textbooks per started eight course weeks,200-peso ID, optional3,000-peso Manila or Clark pickup, and a refundable3,000-peso room deposit excluded from totals.
