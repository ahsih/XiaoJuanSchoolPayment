### JIC universal registration waiver (2026-10-19)

All students quoted through Sida Qihang for JIC Challenger or JIC Premium receive a full waiver of the one-time school registration fee, regardless of study duration. The calculator and generated quote image must show the original registration charge and an equal negative `思达启航注册费减免` line so the net registration amount is zero for every new student; a returning student's registration row is 0 with the template's 老学员返校免费 note and no waiver line (2026-11-23). `cloneJicContentConfig` upgrades legacy published JIC rules that limited the waiver to returning, extension, or 12-week students; do not restore a week threshold or allow stale published content to disable this waiver.

Baguio JIC uses `jic-pricing.ts` and `jic-student-quote.ts` for its confirmed Challenger/Premium course, room, promotion and local-fee rules; keep them synchronized with `SeedJicPricingAsync`. The user's 2026-11 school pack (2026-09 orientation deck, Pricelist_202605, the 2026-06-19 offer table, the 2025-07 local-fee sheets, the 2027 calendar and the BESA pickup poster) is authoritative over earlier screenshots and published employee content: `JIC_SCHOOL_PACK_VERSION` (`applyJicSchoolPack` in `jic-content-config.ts`) moves its course, room and local-fee facts into a saved document once, after which prices and notes edited in the employee editor stay (2026-11-26; before, every load restored the pack, so edited JIC course prices never reached the page), and the editor no longer copies database prices into a JIC document's first save; 2027 prices equal 2026. Course `displayName` values are bilingual and shared by the public table, quote selector and quote image; `name` remains the database lookup value. 1/2/3 weeks cost 40%/65%/85% of the four-week course and room rate; longer stays are proportional. Four-weekly maintenance (1,200 pesos, user-confirmed 2026-11-24; the 2025-07 sheets said 1,000), utilities (3,000) and laundry (1,200, charged even if unused) have a one-period minimum and use proportional extra weeks (six weeks is 1.5 periods). Promotions: off-season offers count study weeks, not the arrival date: room weeks wholly inside a window and not in a peak week are grouped by four, each complete four weeks earning quad 150 / twin and single 50 in 2026/08/23–2027/01/09, and quad 100 / twin 50 / only the Premium 单人雅房 50 among singles in 2027/02/21–06/26 and 2027/08/22–2028/01/08; the holiday offer (200 dollars once for 2026/11/29, 12/13, 12/27 and 2027/01/10 arrivals, 150 for 2027/11/28–2028/01/09 term starts, twin or quad, at least four weeks) needs a term-start arrival; neither applies to in-school extensions; long-term tiers 12/16/20/24 weeks earn 300/400/500/600 dollars and stack. The BESA registration offer has closed, so the page asks no registration date. All Sida applicants receive the full registration-fee waiver (see the waiver section). Peak surcharge is 40 dollars per unique overlapping study/accommodation week during 2026/06/28–08/22 and 60 during the BESA 2027/06/27–08/22 range; peak weeks earn no off-season credit and the surcharge never participates in discounts. Applied surcharges and discounts are school-payment rows and are not repeated as a generic image footer catalog. Each student chooses either a 30-day tourist visa (default) or a 59-day tourist visa; never label either as visa-free. A 30-day tourist visa needs one extension for each started four weeks beyond week 4, a 59-day visa for each started four weeks beyond week 8 (user rule 2026-11-24: 59-day stays up to 8 weeks need none). Extension increments are 4,940/6,210/4,240/4,240/4,240 pesos; a 59-day visa's first extension is the school's second one (6,210). ACR I-CARD is 4,000 pesos once with the first required extension. SSP is 7,800, SSP E-CARD 4,500, student ID 200, Sunday BESA pickup is 3,000 pesos from Manila or Clark (Clark: Sunday 12:00 and 17:00 and the Monday 01:30 van, bay 3), and refundable deposit 3,000 stays outside totals. Textbooks are course-specific per started four weeks; Speaking Pro has no amount in the latest local-fee table and must remain zero with a confirmation warning rather than borrowing another course's price. IELTS Guarantee has IELTS Standard's tuition, needs at least 8 consecutive weeks (shorter plans are blocked) and adds 18,000 pesos per started eight weeks. Terms start every second Sunday from 2026/11/29; new quotes start on the next term Sunday, and each arrival or leaving off the term dates shows the 8,000-peso reference fee below the local fees. Webpage and full-detail image share the same calculated rows and notes.
