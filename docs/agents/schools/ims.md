## IMS Academy quote and employee-content rules

IMS at `/philippines-study/cebu/ims-academy` renders `ImsSchoolPageComponent` on the shared Philippine template in CIA's compact layout with `layoutVersion: 2` quote and comparison images (2026-11-25, from IMS's IMS-CHINA Drive pack and its 2026-11-19 notice on the off-season 2+2 offer). `ims-page-content.ts` holds all page text and data, `ims-page-profile.ts` the profile, `ims-page-quote.ts` the student calculation (`ImsPageStudent`, `ImsQuote`, `ImsPlanComparison`) and `ims-content-config.ts` (code `IMS`) the versioned employee document built from the price-list transcription in `ims-pricing.ts`; the old `ims-school.component.*` and `ims-student-quote.ts` are gone. Prices come only from the content document (`applyPricingData` is a no-op; `SeedImsPricingAsync` stays the insert-only baseline). `IMS_POLICY_VERSION` 1 moves a saved document once onto the new names, walk-in choice, local fees and offers and keeps saved prices; `cloneImsContentConfig` keeps `tuition2027` equal to the editor's `tuition` because IMS has one price year. 2027 prices are not published, so quotes use the 2026 list with a note. Courses use their four-week price, 1/2/3 weeks at 30/60/80%; IELTS and TOEIC Guarantee are 12/16/20/24-week packages (`feeByWeeks`, listed per 12 weeks). Registration (100 dollars) is charged to every student, returning students too (the template's `hideReturning` hides the choice and `registrationBaseNote` replaces the row note). Order (user rule): the 思达启航 5% on the full tuition and room first, except the four 2+2 weeks, which the school's notice gives no 95折; then the 2+2 offer (opt-in under 学生情况: the first four off-season weeks of one course and one room cost their two-week price; tasks: three notes and one 1–2-minute video on 小红书 or 抖音 by Friday 12:00 of week 3, and an interview video in the last week), the off-season offer (75 dollars per off-season course week outside the 2+2 block, 300 per four weeks, from 4 course weeks) and the long-stay tier on paid course weeks (2+2 counts its four weeks as two; 8/12/16/20/24 → 50/100/200/300/400). A week belongs to the month of its Wednesday; January, June, July and August are not off-season, and the calendar marks those 2027 weeks through `peakSeasonsWithoutSurcharge` (no surcharge); for such school-only ranges the template marks a month only when a covered class week has its Wednesday in it, so the week from 2027/05/30 marks June, not May (HANA unchanged). Ages: most courses 15+, TOEIC Speaking, OPIc, TESOL and Parents ESL 20+, Senior ESL 50+, Junior ESL 6/8/9 for 7–14 with a parent on Parents ESL; minors may add Guardian Service (450 dollars per 4 weeks, off-season only) or School Management (2,100). Local fees: SSP 8,000, E-CARD 4,000, visa extensions 5,050/6,800/4,450… from week 5 on a 30-day visa or 6,800/4,450… from week 9 on a 59-day visa, ACR I-CARD 4,000 with the first extension, books 3,000 per course type, ID 300, water and electricity 500 and air-conditioning 250 per room week; walk-in students (room `walk-in`, 0 dollars, no 校内／校外 badge) pay none of the room fees or deposit. Deposit 3,000, pickup and drop-off 1,000, walk-in lunch and extra classes are reference rows outside the total. The refund section has the 思达启航 deposit rows and the school's post-entry rules only. Never publish the agency refund account, agency-only rules, commission, the 2022 business permit, addresses, phone numbers or social accounts. Media: `public/assets/ims/campus/`, `rooms/` and `videos/` (the Drive pack's four clips plus three student interviews and the Senior ESL class from IMS's official YouTube channel, in the hero album's 校园／学员访谈／课程 groups).
