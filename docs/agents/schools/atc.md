## Schools outside the Philippines: shared template and ATC (2026-11-24)

User request 2026-11-24: non-Philippine school pages had no fixed layout, so they are built with a sales mindset (trust, conversion, consultations) on one shared template in `src/app/pages/overseas-study-tour/west-school-page/`. `west-school-profile.ts` is the pure content contract (`WestSchoolProfile`) and `WestSchoolPageComponent` (`app-west-school-page`) renders it in a fixed order: hero with a price anchor, the two calls to action (算一算我的费用, 加顾问微信咨询) and photo buttons → time-limited offers (`WestPromo.until`, hidden afterwards, with days left) → why this school (accreditation badges) → campus choice → videos (`preload="none"`) → course cards and price tabs → accommodation (a card without a sure photo shows an icon) → the school's own quote calculator, projected with `<app-xxx-quote-calculator quote>` → every other fee → an optional feature block (e.g. study & work) → weekly and monthly activities → optional junior block → calendar → who it suits → why 思达启航 (picked from `BRAND_ADVANTAGES`) → consultant cards in today's order → FAQ → closing band, with a sticky section bar and a fixed phone bar (电话／微信咨询／免费报价). Every content section ends with `SCHOOL_REFERENCE_NOTE`. A school only writes a profile, a pricing module, a quote engine that builds the shared `UkQuoteSheet` (`currencyLabel` names the currency) and a small calculator; it never copies the template. Burlington still has its own older page.

ATC Language Schools (Ireland) is the first school on it: `/overseas-study-tour/ireland/atc-language-schools`, files in `src/app/pages/overseas-study-tour/ireland-schools/atc/` (`atc-pricing.ts` 2027 euro prices, `atc-quote.ts` engine, `atc-page-content.ts` profile, `atc-quote-calculator.component.*`, `atc-school.component.ts`), media in `public/assets/atc/`. Prices are compiled (no seed, no content revision), shown in 欧元 at the school's price with no 思达启航 discount. Bray takes students from 16 and Dublin from 17; 16–17-year-olds stay only in a host-family twin room, at most 4 weeks from September to May, and must book the arrival transfer. Courses start any 2027 Monday except public holidays and the Christmas closure (2027-12-18–2028-01-03; the stay grows by the two closed weeks). Every week is charged at the tier of the total weeks; MediPel is required from 13 weeks; TIE is free from 25 weeks. Black November (10% off tuition, bookings by 2026-11-30) and the Renewal Visa package (by 2026-11-30, start by 2027-01-31) disappear from the page and the quote after 2026-11-30. Never publish the school's phone, email, website, social accounts, addresses or postcodes, agency terms or named testimonials. Sources, decisions and open questions: `docs/atc-source-2026-11-24.md`. Tests: `npx ng test --watch=false --browsers=ChromeHeadlessNoSandbox --ts-config=tsconfig.atc.spec.json --include=src/app/pages/overseas-study-tour/ireland-schools/atc/atc-quote.spec.ts --progress=false`.
