CELLA Uni Sparta and CELLA Premium share `cella-pricing.ts`, `cella-student-quote.ts`, and the standalone `CellaQuoteCalculatorComponent`; keep these sources synchronized with `SeedCellaPricingAsync`. Each campus route has its own campus-locked calculator and quote image: never expose a cross-campus selector or mix Uni and Premium students in one quote. Each route supports single people and independent 2–20-person groups, with course, room, Sunday start date, enrollment status and promotion per student. Premium also offers the ten 2027 Family Package combinations (campus dormitory or STACIA HOTEL; 1–3 guardians and children per twin, triple or quad family room) as course choices. Their 1/2/3/4-week prices are exact whole-family totals that already include the 500-dollar family registration (school answer 2026-11-21: 5,900 dollars is the whole amount for one guardian and one child for four weeks), course, accommodation, meals and the listed local fees; never add ordinary room, registration, pickup, laundry or local-fee rows again, do not infer promotion stacking, and block stays over four weeks because the table has no price for them. The STACIA package has no guardian class. The 2026/08/30–2027/01/02 low-season offer deducts 100 dollars once per complete four-week eligible room period, never once from both course and room. Premium six-person accommodation uses 499 dollars per four weeks in the window instead of 600, can stack with the 8/12/16-week long-stay deductions of 50/100/150 dollars, and cannot receive the low-season 100-dollar deduction. Social 6+2 and 9+3 offers use actual stays of 8/12 weeks while charging 6/9 weeks of both course and quad accommodation; local fees always use actual weeks. These offers are new-student-only, combine only with the 思达启航9折 and a Christmas offer, never with any other school promotion (CELLA staff confirmation 2026-11-21), and page/image must retain the complete Xiaohongshu/Douyin obligations and recovery warning. Do not enable the old Christmas/New Year offer. 思达启航折扣 (user decision 2026-11-21, `CELLA_SIDA_RATE`, content rule `cella-sida`): every ordinary CELLA quote takes 10% off course and room after every school offer, fixed amounts first and the six-person 25% next, as CELLA's own formula “(学费＋住宿费) − 圣诞优惠，× 0.75 × 中介9折 ＋ 注册费” states; registration, the summer surcharge and the minor fee are not discounted. Family packages also take the 9折 (user decision 2026-11-21) on the package price less the 500-dollar registration it holds (`CELLA_FAMILY_REGISTRATION_FEE`). The Christmas amount in that formula is not in the calculator because the 2027 Christmas offer has not been supplied. Optional pickup, laundry and room-deposit references must not be blindly multiplied for groups. All CELLA website and image money labels use Chinese currency names.
