# School notes: philinter

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-11-26 to keep that file small. Record new decisions about this topic here; AGENTS.md holds only rules that apply to every task.

Philinter (`/philippines-study/cebu/philinter-academy`, school code `PHILINTER`) moved onto the shared Philippine template on 2026-11-24 (user request, from the school's Google Drive pack “Philinter 2026-2027”: 2026 and 2027 tuition sheets, 2026-10-19 dormitory and Azon local-fee sheets, 2026-10-19 student handbook, new-student guides, 2027 holiday poster, permit, immigration accreditation and TESDA registration). The route renders `PhilinterSchoolPageComponent` in CIA's compact layout with `layoutVersion: 2` images; `philinter-page-content.ts` holds the page text and data, `philinter-page-profile.ts` the profile and `philinter-page-quote.ts` the student calculation. The old catalog, quote and detail component were removed, and this pack supersedes the earlier Philinter rules (12–17 juniors, family prepayment, the 26,700-peso example, the disabled 2027 summer estimate). `philinter-content-config.ts` (`PHILINTER_POLICY_VERSION`, upgrades saved documents once and keeps edited text) has 19 bookable courses (Focus Industry is disabled: only by interview after arrival) and six rooms with `fee` (2026) and `fee2027`; course prices are equal in both years. The 2027 sheet says “搶鮮看，尚未實施” and the school has not said when it takes effect, so every student pays the 2026 prices (rooms `fee`, 120-dollar registration) whether they arrive in 2026 or 2027 (user decision 2026-11-24); the price list, room note and FAQ say so. The 2027 room prices and 150-dollar registration stay in the content document (`fee2027`, `registrationFee2027`) and apply only once `priceSwitchArrival` is set to the first Sunday arrival the school names; it is empty until then. Prices come only from the content document (`applyPricingData` is a no-op). Fixed lengths (`PHILINTER_FIXED_WEEKS`): IELTS Guarantee 8 or 12 weeks, the two 16-week IELTS packages 16 (shown as 4,430 / 4,260 dollars per 16 weeks), Premium Speaking 8, Premium Junior Speaking 4–6, Booster Speaking 4; TOEIC Guarantee at least 12; Booster Speaking and PTE start only from 2027-01-03. Junior courses only for under-18s (Junior ESL 7–17, Junior IELTS and Junior Speaking 12–17) and never in a single room; the guardian takes Light ESL. 1–3 weeks cost 45/65/85%. Offers: 思达启航 10% on tuition and accommodation first, then the 2026 low-season offer (300 dollars per 8 consecutive study weeks in a campus triple or Azon single/twin, 2026/08/16–12/25, no guarantee courses); returning students waive registration. Peak surcharge 40 dollars per unique week in 2026/07/05–08/29 and 60 in 2027/07/04–08/28 (CALA), outside every discount; a summer stay under 8 dormitory or 4 Azon weeks only adds a note, because shorter stays need school agreement. Local fees follow the 2026-10-19 sheets exactly (SSP 7,800, E-card 4,500, ID 400, management 600, water 250 and electricity 1,000 per room week; Azon water 450/650/850 for 1–3 weeks and electricity 1,500 more once; extensions from week 9 on a 59-day entry or week 5 on a 30-day entry: 6,920 / 4,440 / 4,950 / 5,450 / 5,450, with ACR I-card 4,000 and ARP 300 at the first one); textbooks are an estimate of 2,000 pesos per course type per started 4 weeks; the deposit (2,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 / 5,000 by stay length) and pickup (1,200 weekend, 1,500 weekday or red-eye) stay outside the total. The page shows a licence strip (text only), a refund section from the handbook plus the 思达启航 deposit rule, and the 2027 weekday holidays; never publish the handbook's nationality chart, Wi-Fi passwords, sensitive-topic list or its rule that only the original agency may extend. Media come from the pack and the school's own channels: room photos in `public/assets/philinter/rooms/` (each labelled by the room it surely shows), other photos in `gallery/`, and twenty 720p H.264 clips with `<name>-poster.webp` posters in `videos/` (about 212 MB; hero album groups 校园／宿舍／课程／活动／学员访谈). Eight of them were added on 2026-11-25 from the school's official YouTube channel (its Facebook page has the same videos): a 2026 drone tour, a school overview, the international staff, an IELTS course introduction and four interviews (user request: trust-building videos). Clips were cut before social-media handles, a website card, shop signs and an email address, or the handles were removed with ffmpeg delogo. Sunny's interview ends before its outreach scene. Channel reviews labelled Taiwan or showing handles throughout were not used. The “From Taiwan” bar in the pronunciation interview is covered with a gray mosaic; clips and photos showing the Taiwan authorities' flag, another company's logo or a contact line were left out, as was the outreach video (children's faces). The older top-level photos that did not show what their names said were deleted; `campus-main`, `campus-pool`, `condo-room` (the Azon tower) and `group-classroom` (home page and SEO) remain.
