# School notes: cboa

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-11-26 to keep that file small. Record new decisions about this topic here; AGENTS.md holds only rules that apply to every task.

Cebu Blue Ocean Academy (蓝海CBOA, `/philippines-study/cebu/cebu-blue-ocean-academy`) moved onto the shared Philippine template in CIA's compact layout on 2026-11-23 (user request, from the school's November 2026 pack: 2027 price list, 2027 promotion sheet, 2027 local-fee sheet, brochure, minor-student notice and Ocean Suites media; the Pines files in the same pack were ignored). `CboaSchoolPageComponent` (`cebu-blue-ocean-school/cboa-school-page.component.ts`) uses `cboa-page-content.ts` (all page text and data), `cboa-page-profile.ts` and `cboa-page-quote.ts` (`CboaPageStudent`, `CboaQuote`, `CboaPlanComparison`, all with `layoutVersion: 2`); the old `cebu-blue-ocean-school.component` and `blue-ocean-student-quote.ts` are gone. `cboa-content-config.ts` is the versioned employee document; prices come only from it (`applyPricingData` is a no-op), and `CBOA_POLICY_VERSION` 1 replaces a saved document's catalogue, local fees, offers and peak dates once while keeping media and edited image lines. Tuition, rooms and offers follow the **registration date**: before 2027/02/01 the 2026 prices (`tuition`/`fee`) and 2026 offers, from it the 2027 prices (`tuition2027`/`fee2027`) and 2027 offers; local fees follow the **entry date** (`futureEffectiveStart` 2027-02-01). Until 2027/02/01 the course and room lists show the 2026 price, with the 2027 price inside each opened course. EBE (高管商务英语) and the Ocean Suites Couple Room (情侣房, 950 dollars per person per four weeks, two people booking together) exist only in the 2027 list, so a 2026 registration choosing them is blocked. 1/2/3 weeks are 40/65/85% of four weeks, longer stays proportional; Survival ESL at most 4 weeks; the Senior Course is for 40+. Every Sida applicant waives the 100-dollar registration and gets 95% on gross tuition and accommodation. 2026 offers (registration by 2027/01/31): 150 dollars per complete four off-peak course weeks, 100 at 12+ course weeks, 100 at 16 weeks plus 25 per further two weeks. 2027 offers (registration 2027/02/01–06/30 and entry from 2027/02/01): 25 dollars per off-peak study week, and 100 at 12 course weeks plus 25 per further week (peak weeks count). The peak uses the school's own dates from its latest notice (the 2027 promotion sheet) at 60 dollars per unique study/accommodation week, 2027/06/27–08/21 (the past 2026 range was removed, user decision 2026-11-24), outside every discount; family courses (Junior 7–15, Parents) have no peak price and block the quote there. From 2026/08/23 entry, students under 18 may take only the Junior family course. Local fees: SSP 7,800, SSP I-CARD 4,500, ACR I-CARD 4,000 with the first visa extension (user decision 2026-11-24: a 30-day visa for 6 weeks pays it, a 59-day visa for 8 weeks does not), visa extensions from week 5 for a 30-day visa (5,140 / 6,410 / 4,440…) or from week 9 for a 59-day visa (6,410 / 4,440…), management 500 a week (700 for entries from 2027/02/01), Ocean Suites electricity and water 1,000 a week, textbooks per course type per started four weeks (estimates, settled at actual purchase), student ID 300; long-term visas zero the visa rows with an adviser note. The 1,000-peso weekly deposit, EGI electricity and water by meter (deducted from the deposit), pickup 1,200 / 1,500, drop-off 1,000 and laundry 200 are reference rows outside the total. A folded refund section shows the school's post-entry rule (the first two weeks are not refunded, the rest at 70%), the 300-dollar non-refundable family-camp deposit and the shared 思达启航 deposit rules; the agency payment deadline, visit and commission rules are not published. Photos and videos are the school's own Ocean Suites and campus media in `public/assets/cebu-blue-ocean/` (the tour clip's bank ATM shot was cut). Since 2026-11-26 five clips from the school's official YouTube channel add 校园 (the 2025 campus tour, without its flag row, which held the Taiwan authorities' flag, a test-company logo, the café counter and a QR-code notice board, plus 2024 one-to-one and self-study shorts) and 学员 chips (a Chinese student's interview and a Chinese vlog without the smoking area and the shops outside the gate); details in `docs/cboa-update-2026-11-23.md`. The EGI dorm photos (`rooms/egi-01…03.webp`) are the brochure's three embedded room photos at native size (only 281, 144 and 134 px wide), placed under all three EGI price cards at the user's request (2026-11-24) until the school checks them and sends originals; replace them when it does. Four more clips from the same channel were added on 2026-11-27 (user choice): airport pickup and first day (in the 校园 chip), textbooks and classes and a teacher interview (a new 课程 chip), and `egi-rooms`, the tour's own twin and triple room shots, which is in the 房型 chip and opens the EGI room album. They were cut clear of a fast-food logo and sign, taxi-company cars, a third-party publisher's book, the IELTS and PINES logos, QR codes, a filled-in form and certificates with students' names.
