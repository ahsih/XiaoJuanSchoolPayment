### B'Cebu confirmed discount order (2026-10-19)

B'Cebu applies fixed long-stay savings first, then the reporter-activity deduction, then the applicable off-season percentage, and finally the Sida/agent percentage. Both fixed deductions must reduce the percentage-discount base. For the confirmed 12-week example, Speed ESL 4 weeks (900 dollars), Business English 8 weeks (2,100 dollars), and a triple room 12 weeks (2,250 dollars) total 5,250 dollars; subtract 100 long-stay and 300 reporter savings, apply 15% off and then 10% off, producing 3,710.25 dollars. The webpage, generated image, prepayment and post-activity refund must use this same order.
