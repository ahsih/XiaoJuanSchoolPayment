## HANA Academy, Clark (2026-11-23)

`/philippines-study/clark/hana-academy` renders `HanaSchoolPageComponent` on the shared Philippine template in CIA's compact layout with `layoutVersion: 2` images. Page text is in the pure `hana-school/hana-page-content.ts`, prices and rules in `hana-content-config.ts` (2026 four-week prices; no database lessons, so `applyPricingData` is a no-op) and the student calculation in `hana-page-quote.ts`. HANA has no peak surcharge: its peak seasons (2026/6/28–8/29, 2027/1/3–2/27, 2027/6/27–8/28) are only marked on the calendar through the template's optional `peakSeasonsWithoutSurcharge`. The low-season long-stay discount (10/11.5/13/14.5% by low-season weeks, senior 10%) and the adult “付4周读8周” offer (Light ESL + double room, 1,470 dollars for 8 weeks; never combined with other discounts or an added agency discount, school rule) are calculated per student. The junior camp is an all-inclusive season price (Camp B weeks after the fourth cost its 4-week price ÷ 4, user decision 2026-11-24) and the senior course includes local fees except the deposit. Visa extensions follow the user's rule for every Philippine school: one extension per extra 30 days, from week 5 on a 30-day visa and from week 9 on a 59-day visa. From 2027-01-01 the quote is blocked until HANA publishes 2027 prices. Reviews are short excerpts of public reviews without names (the template's optional `textReviews` cards). `schoolCode: 'HANA'` is cast because the code union lives in the locked CIA files; HANA has no employee content editor yet. Media are in `public/assets/hana/`. Videos (2026-11-25, user request for more trust-building videos) are the school film, a native-teacher interview and four of the school's own 2026 Facebook reels (campus tour, dormitory tour, children's cooking class, a family interview), grouped in the hero album as 校园／宿舍／外教／活动／访谈; each room album opens with its part of the dormitory tour (`HANA_ROOM_VIDEOS`), and the single and family room photos are stills from that tour. A staff-name door sign and a frame with the school website and named certificates are covered by frozen frames; outings to other businesses, clips with the flag of the Taiwan authorities or Taiwan listed as a country, and test-company logos were left out (the template gives a room card a 看照片 button only when a room profile matches it; the 乐龄夫妻房 has none). School answers of 2026-11-24 and the revised Chinese orientation: both golf courses run (700 and 1,000 dollars per 4 weeks, from 2 weeks, practice range and balls included, course play extra); the refund section uses the 思达启航 deposit rows instead of the school's pre-entry penalties, the post-entry 50% rule counted from dorm check-in, the “付4周读8周” rule counted on paid weeks and the no-refund cases; the 400-dollar family peak booking deposit from summer 2027 is optional for agencies, so it stays off the page.
