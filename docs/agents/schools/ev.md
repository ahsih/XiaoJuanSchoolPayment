# School notes: ev

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-11-26 to keep that file small. Record new decisions about this topic here; AGENTS.md holds only rules that apply to every task.

EV Academy quotes allow separate course/accommodation segments within one synchronized stay of up to 24 weeks per student. The shared `SchoolQuotePlan.syncSchedules` option is enabled for all ordinary school quotes: changing either first arrival date moves both lists, changing weeks or adding/removing a segment synchronizes both totals, and later start dates automatically follow the preceding Saturday end. Preserve earlier types/durations in the other list and adjust its final occupied segment; trim segments outside a shortened stay. Course and room segment counts may differ (for example, one four-week course with one week in a triple and three in a quad). Preserve EV's existing 40%/65%/85% genuine short-stay ratios, 95% course/accommodation price, minor fee and peak-season rules. Registration is charged once. Management and utilities use accommodation weeks, textbooks use course weeks, and visa/document estimates use the full stay. Mixed campus/off-campus stays show and calculate both management-fee rows from their respective accumulated accommodation weeks. Invalid/overlapping rows block image export. Quote images use CG's dynamically sized school-payment rows for every selected course/accommodation, including names, dates, weeks and course schedules. Use the exact image heading pattern “EV主校区 {累计课程周数}周报价” (with a space, like CIA since the 2026-11-21 template), including multi-row quotes; omit the subtitle, “组合” label and stay-span recaps. Stay span remains an internal calculation input, not a displayed summary. Keep local-fee introductory text, all fee notes and displayed currency rounding identical to the webpage; image footnotes omit internal pricing/synchronization algorithms; preserve necessary discount and surcharge conditions in their payment rows.

For EV, once one student's own total course duration reaches four weeks, every course and accommodation segment uses its own published four-week price divided by four and multiplied by that segment's weeks, regardless of changes in course or room type. Never apply the 1/2/3-week short-stay ratios to those split segments. Do not add course and room durations together, count gaps as paid weeks, or combine different students to reach the threshold. Genuine under-four-week quotes retain the configured short-stay ratios. Webpage rows and generated-image rows share `EvStudentCalculator` pricing; image notes must not expose internal duration-tier or synchronization algorithms.

## EV main campus update (2026-11-19)

The user supplied the September 2026 EV pack (price list dated 2026-04-13, 2026-08-10 dormitory and condo local-fee sheets, 2026 Chinese brochure, orientation guide and rules). Tuition and room prices did not change. `ev-content-config.ts` now follows the 2026-08-10 sheets, and `quoteSettings.evPolicyVersion` upgrades older saved documents once while keeping edited prices:

- Management is 500 pesos per week for the dorm and 1,000 per week for the condo; electricity is 500 and water 300 per week. All are charged by accommodation week, not in 4-week blocks.
- ACR I-Card is 4,000, charged with the first tourist-visa extension (see the colleague review below). ARP (300 pesos, once at the first tourist-visa extension or for a long-term visa) is not on the sheets but the user confirmed it is charged; keep it.
- Visa extensions start at week 5 (30-day visa, cumulative 5,430/11,830/16,260/20,690/25,120) or week 9 (59-day visa, 6,700/11,130/15,560/19,990), then every 4 weeks (`rates` and `tourist59Rates`).
- The refundable deposit is 2,000 for 1–3 weeks, 3,000 for 4–8 and 5,000 from 9 weeks.
- IELTS Guarantee (雅思保分班) starts at 8 weeks. Its whole-course tuition for 8–11 weeks is 2,700/2,987.5/3,275/3,562.5 dollars (`feeByWeeks`); from 12 weeks it is 1,290 per 4 weeks.
- The off-campus condo is Semi-Sparta only, and the calculator blocks condo weeks that overlap a Sparta course.
