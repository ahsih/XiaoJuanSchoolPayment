## CIEC page on the template (2026-11-23)

CIEC stays at `/philippines-study/cebu/ciec`. Since the 2026-11-23 school pack (2027 Chinese brochure, 2027 tuition sheet `v.270101`, 2027 low-season offer, new-student guide, 2027 holiday poster) the route renders `CiecSchoolPageComponent` on the shared Philippine template in CIA's compact layout, with `layoutVersion: 2` quote and comparison images. `ciec-content-config.ts` is CIEC's content document (2027 prices equal 2026; registration 100 dollars per person, returning students free; 2/3 weeks at 65/85%, one week not offered), `ciec-page-content.ts` holds all page text and data, `ciec-page-profile.ts` fills the template and `ciec-page-quote.ts` holds the calculation. Prices come only from the content document (`applyPricingData` is a no-op; the database has only the regional starting-price row `菲律宾宿务CIEC`). The old `ciec-quote.ts`, its calculator component and `ciec-quote.spec.ts` are gone.

Each person in a quote is a child with a parent, a parent (家长／监护人) or a child without a parent (`role` and `childAge` in the template's `extraChoices`; `hideAgeGroup` hides the template's age select); a group quote is one family, student 2 defaults to the parent. Children under 4 (`m24`, `m47`) take 不上课: up to 24 months no room and board, 25–47 months half, neither counts as a room occupant, but management and water are charged and registration is per person. Kindergarten is 4–6 and needs a parent; junior courses start at 6. Parents take a parent course unless the stay is over 12 weeks, a child under 4 comes along, or a low-season family offer applies (tuition sheet conditions). Children without a parent pay the 300-dollar/4-week guardian fee. CIDEC adds a one-time 3,000-dollar grade tuition (inside the Sida 95%), needs at least 4 weeks and uses its own 15,000-peso SSP + E-CARD. Optional Cebu pickup is 20 dollars per person.

Low-season offers (study days Monday–Friday inside 2026/08/31–2027/01/01, 2027/03/01–05/21 or 2027/08/30–12/31, at least 4 registered weeks, applied per eligible week): a 1 parent + 1 child family in a double room pays 300 (2026) or 400 (2027) dollars per 4 weeks for the parent's room; 1 parent + 2 children in a triple room, the parent's room is free; parent courses drop to 400 (ESL) or 500 (Business/IELTS); children 9+ without a parent pay no guardian fee; primary-school children get a non-cash craft class in the 2026 window. The Sida 95% applies to the full tuition, room and CIDEC price first and these offers come off after it (user rule 2026-11-24: CIEC's files give no order), except a parent room the school makes free, which stays out of the 5%. In the low season a 2-person family may take a triple room when one is free: room `triple-family2` (三人间（2人家庭升级）, 1,130 dollars = double 930 + 200 per 4 weeks for each person, so the parent pays the low-season price + 200); both must stay in it together, only in low-season weeks; 方案对比 leaves this room and the four-person room out. The four-person triple price (770) needs exactly four non-infant family members in that room. No peak surcharge; peak months (6–8, 12–2) take a 500-dollar family deposit, explained on the page.

Local fees follow the 2027 sheet: SSP 8,000 and E-CARD 4,500 for everyone who studies, I-CARD 4,000 with the first visa extension, visa extensions 4,800 / 5,800 / 5,300 / 5,500 / 5,500 / 4,000 (a 30-day entry first extends 29 days to day 59; a 59-day entry starts with the second step), management 2,400 and water 1,200 per person per 4 weeks, group or kindergarten copies 1,000 per 4 course weeks, ID 200; books, electricity (23 pesos/kWh), deposit, kindergarten kit, sending and extra nights are reference rows. Long-term visas zero the visa rows with the adviser note. CRTV and ECC (over 6 months) are outside the 24-week quote. Photos are the brochure's own, at native size in `public/assets/ciec/` (no upscaling); the hero and quote image use `campus-entrance.webp`. Videos (2026-11-25, user request for more trust-building clips) are six clips from CIEC's own YouTube channels (CIEC GLOBAL and the Korean CIDEC CIEC channel) in `public/assets/ciec/videos/` (720p H.264 with posters, about 120 MB), listed in `CIEC_VIDEOS` and grouped in the hero album as 校园／课堂／夏令营／访谈: the 2022 school tour (its logo cards with a “best junior school” slogan cut), a pronunciation class, a 2020 STEAM class (stock footage and subscribe card cut), a 2026 summer-camp story with Vietnamese captions and two students talking about their teachers in English. Talks only in Vietnamese, airport-only clips and 2018–2019 footage of the campus before it was repainted were left out; Facebook was not checked because it needs a login. The 2027 holiday table lists weekday holidays only. Six real CIEC test reports (user decision 2026-11-24) are in `public/assets/ciec/test-results/` with student names and the school's address and phone pixelated; they use the template's results block through the optional item fields `summary` and `kind` (internal tests, so never labelled 官方雅思). The refund section (user decision 2026-11-24) follows the new-student guide, plus 思达启航's own rule: the 2,000-yuan deposit paid when registering is never refunded (a change of start date at least 4 weeks before entry, or keeping the deposit for one year, is allowed), and late rescheduling may cost a penalty.
