## UK schools and Burlington School (2026-11-19)

UK school quotes differ from the Philippine ones (weekly all-inclusive packages, tiered tuition, accommodation and one-time fees in pounds; no peso local fees), so they use a separate shared template that every future UK school should reuse:

- `src/app/components/uk-quote/uk-quote-sheet.ts` is the pure `UkQuoteSheet` contract: 1–2 compared plans, required fee rows with totals and renminbi estimates, `{{emphasis}}` include rows, optional reference rows (never totalled), notes, consultant and blocking `issues`. `UkQuoteSheetComponent` renders it as the web table; `uk-quote-image-renderer.ts` draws the matching image (the user-approved layout: brand header, title band, 01 必付费用, 02 费用包含, 03 可选费用, 04 报价说明, consultant QR card, footer). `QuoteImageDownloadButtonComponent` draws it when `layout: 'uk-package'` and `ukSheet` are set, reusing the existing save/preview/WeChat behaviour; the Philippine `cia-detailed` template is untouched. Keep both renderer and sheet free of Angular, DOM, storage and regex lookbehind.
- Exchange rates: read `rates.GBP` from `ExchangeRateService.getLatestCnyRates()` (Frankfurter/ECB); a missing rate blocks image export. Never use a fixed pound rate.

Burlington School (London) is at `/overseas-study-tour/uk/burlington-school`, under 海外游学 → 英国游学 in the menu, and the UK tour page lists it through the optional `schools` field of the shared study-tour page content. Files are in `src/app/pages/overseas-study-tour/uk-schools/burlington-school/`: `burlington-pricing.ts` (every 2027 price from the school's 12-page Price List 2027), `burlington-quote.ts` (pure engine → `UkQuoteSheet`), `burlington-page-content.ts` (all page text, mini-program-ready), the page component (CIA/CIEC layout inside the `<app-cia-school>` styling wrapper) and the calculator component. Photos in `public/assets/burlington/` come from the school's partner marketing library (Google Drive zips received 2026-11-19: school, iQ Vega, Bath castle and Young Learner photos), exported at most 1400 px wide; only the castle firepit and outdoor-cinema images are still crops from the price-list PDF. Skip the brochure's Unsplash stock images, the "Partner Events" photos (agent fam trips) and the agent webinars. The eight complete videos in `public/assets/burlington/videos/` (about 110 MB) were transcoded to 720p H.264 with Windows' built-in media transcoder, each with a poster frame, and are played with `preload="none"` so nothing downloads until a visitor presses play; keep the original files out of the repository. Junior timetable, curfew rules, project topics, homestay vetting, iQ Vega facilities and Wandsworth apartment details come from the 2026 agent presentations and the Spring 2027 junior programme. Where those older documents conflict with the 2027 price list (for example the £75 versus £80 Day Programme Saturday trip, or 2026 castle prices), the price list wins. User decision 2026-11-19: public copy and quotes never cite where information came from (no "按价目表", "宣传册数据" or "学校提供的资料" wording), and detailed school rules (timetables, curfew, facilities, room rules, homestay vetting, castle activities) carry a "仅供参考，学校可能随时调整" note (`BURLINGTON_REFERENCE_NOTE` and the shared quote note). Prices are compiled (no database seed, no employee content revision) and shown at the school's list price with no Sida discount (user decision).

Quote rules: junior packages (11–17) price `weekly package × weeks + £55 registration` per season (spring 3/14–4/25, summer 5/30–8/29, autumn 10/10–11/7, winter 2028 1/2–1/30), homestay needs age 14+, and the castle is an optional upgrade (£445 summer, £395 other seasons); the free 4-week castle offer applies only to eligible adult courses (confirmed by the school's managing director). Adult courses (16+) use the total-week tuition tier for every week, plus registration, £55 accommodation booking when housing is booked, £45 coursebook per started 12 weeks (estimate), onsite/homestay 16–17 surcharges, the homestay summer supplement for weeks touching 6/13–8/29, apartments 18+ only and homestay Half Board required for 16–17. The Adult Summer Premium Residence (iQ Vega, 6/27–8/22, 2–8 weeks) charges 845/795/745 per week from 2/4/6 weeks plus an optional £80/week intensive upgrade, with registration, materials, booking fee and kitchen pack included. Transfers, travel card, chaperone, castle upgrades, exam and bank charges are optional reference rows only. Agent commission (adults 30% of tuition, summer residence 15%, juniors 20%, groups NET) is internal: never show it on the page, in quotes or images. Never publish the school's phone, email, WhatsApp, social accounts, website or street address/postcode anywhere (page, quote, image, SEO, media), so students enrol through Sida rather than contacting the school directly (user decision 2026-11-19); the area name Balham is fine. The eight videos were checked at their openings and endings and show only the school logo, not contact details; re-check any replacement video. Group packages are NET rates for 15+ students and are not on the public page. Not yet synced to the mini program; follow the mini-program workflow above once the user confirms the layout.
