## HELP Clark page on the template (2026-11-24)

HELP Clark stays at `/philippines-study/clark/help-english-clark` and renders `HelpSchoolPageComponent` on the shared Philippine template in CIA's compact layout, with `layoutVersion: 2` quote and comparison images (school pack from HELP's Google Drive, 2026-11-24; only the Clark material is used). `help-content-config.ts` (code `HELP-CLARK`) is the content document, `help-page-content.ts` holds all page text and data, `help-page-profile.ts` fills the template and `help-page-quote.ts` holds the calculation; prices come only from the content document (`applyPricingData` is a no-op) and `findSchool` picks the Clark database record, never a Baguio campus. The old `help-school.component.*` and `help-school-detail.component.*` are gone.

2026 price list: ESL 900, ESL Intensive, Business, IELTS and TOEIC basic 1,050, IELTS and TOEIC advanced 1,150, and four family-camp courses (guardian, GIS kindergarten, child plan 1 at 1,000 and plan 2 at 1,400) per 4 weeks; single 1,030, twin 780, triple 680, quad 600; 1/2/3 weeks at 40/65/80%; registration 100 dollars, returning students free. 2027 prices are not published, so quotes use 2026 prices with a visible note. Admission Sundays are every two weeks (`HELP_ARRIVAL_DATES_2026`); the default start is the first one on or after the next Sunday. Offers for a first course start 2026-08-16 to 2026-12-06: 100 dollars off-season plus 200 dollars SNS per complete four course weeks, and registration waived from 8 course weeks; then the 思达启航 5% on tuition and accommodation less those offers. No peak surcharge. Minors in an adult course are blocked for any study day in January, February, June, July or August, and otherwise pay the 200-dollar minor management fee; family-camp children do not.

Local fees follow the school's 30/59-day tables (default visa 59 days): SSP 7,800, E-CARD 4,500, extensions 4,940/6,210/4,240 (30-day) or 6,210/4,210 (59-day), ACR I-CARD 4,000 with the first extension, books 1,700 per started 4 course weeks, ID 200, water, electricity, laundry and maintenance 250 each per room week, GIS kindergarten materials 500 per started 4 weeks; long-term visas zero the visa rows with the adviser note. The 3,000-peso deposit and the Manila/Clark pickups (by group size, `helpPickupRate`) are reference rows outside the total. The 2027 summer junior camp is information only, not in the calculator. Photos and room clips are in `public/assets/help-clark/`; the single room has no photo. Two more videos from HELP's own YouTube channels (2026-11-25, Clark only) sit in the hero album: a 2024 student-day vlog (Vietnamese captions) and the 2026 junior-camp recap, cut to drop an airline-wing opening, a visiting group's orange uniform T-shirts with another organisation's logo, and water-park and bowling-alley venue branding.
