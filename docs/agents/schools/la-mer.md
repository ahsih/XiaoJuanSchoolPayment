## EV Academy La Mer independent campus

La Mer is `/philippines-study/cebu/ev-la-mer`, school code `EV-LAMER`, canonical database name `EV Academy La Mer`, and baseline ID `578d438f-2974-4cf9-872c-e67e8253e8da`. It is supported by both unified employee editors and the media review workflow. Never resolve this campus using an ambiguous `EV` match or fall back to the first EV result; the main campus remains separate. Preserve its existing third position in the Cebu directory.

Since 2026-11-23 the route renders `LaMerSchoolPageComponent` (`la-mer-school/la-mer-school-page.component.ts`) on the shared Philippine school template in CIA's compact layout with `layoutVersion: 2` quote and comparison images (user request: update La Mer from the school's 2026-11 pack). `la-mer-page-content.ts` holds all page text and data (from La Mer's 2026-10 Chinese orientation guide, the 2025-12-17 rules, the 2026 brochure and course guide and the 2027 family sheet), `la-mer-page-profile.ts` fills the template, and `la-mer-page-quote.ts` holds the student calculation (`LaMerPageStudent`, `LaMerQuote`, `LaMerPlanComparison`, and `buildLaMerPreviewQuote` for the staff quote-image preview). Family packages are offered as course choices in the quote only (ids `family-<room>-<people>`, never in the course list or 方案对比); choosing one switches the room to the single 家庭房（含在亲子套餐内） choice and collapses the plan to one period. Visa choices are the 30-day (default) and 59-day tourist visas; a family uses one visa choice for every member. The 7-year child minimum is stated in the page text, not asked in the quote. `LA_MER_POLICY_VERSION` 1 moves older saved documents once onto the Chinese course names and lines, the 单人间／多人间 room groups, the ARP row (300 pesos at the first tourist-visa extension, user-confirmed for EV), CIA's company image lines and the new table copy, and drops the old `laMerPage` text; edited values are kept. The page is matched only by `LA_MER_SCHOOL_ID` or the exact name `EV Academy La Mer`. Photos are WebP crops of the school's own pack in `public/assets/la-mer/{campus,study,life,rooms}/` (each room type shows only its own photos), and the three school videos (campus tour, promo film, lunch) are 720p H.264 in `public/assets/la-mer/videos/` with posters. Eight vertical clips filmed on campus by 思达启航 (2026-11-24, the user's `ev lamer.zip`) sit beside them: `pool`, `classes`, `room-pool-double`, `room-double`, `room-quad`, `dining-hall`, `shop` and `gym`; their text starts with 思达启航实拍. The old top-level `assets/la-mer/*.webp` files stay because the Cebu directory, SEO and the server seed still reference some of them. Its actual-page preview uses source/origin-checked `ev-lamer-content-*` messages; uploaded media stays inactive until review and publication.

Confirmed ordinary prices remain the 2025 four-week rates; short stays are 40%/65%/85%. Each student's total course weeks determine their own price tier, with the shared continuous Sunday-to-Saturday multi-segment plan. Ordinary quotes support 1–24 weeks and independent 1–20 people. Registration is 100 dollars once per person and stays outside Sida's 95% course/accommodation rate. The 50-dollar/person/week school offer uses first Monday course start from 2026-08-24 through 2026-12-31, not Sunday arrival, and need not finish within that period. Apply school cash deductions first, then Sida. Ordinary peak surcharge is 40 dollars per overlapping week during 2026-07-05–08-29; 2027 ordinary peak dates are unconfirmed and require a visible qualification.

Family prices are exact 1/2/3/4/6/8-week whole-package totals. The newer 2027-labelled document is effective from 2026-08-23, with 2027-01-03–02-27 and 2027-06-27–08-28 peak stays. User-confirmed 3/4-person compositions allow any mix with at least one guardian and one child. Every member participates in the 50-dollar/week offer when eligible; apply 95% to the remaining entire package, also when the school cash offer is unavailable. Deposit is 500 dollars per member, included in the total and deducted from the balance, not an additional fee. Visa renewals and refundable room deposits are outside the package, calculated per member. Included SSP, E-card, ID, utilities, books and pickup are not charged again. Unlisted durations/rooms or cross-season/version packages block export instead of inventing mixed prices.

`Services/DataInitialize.LaMer.cs` inserts missing ordinary records and a reviewable initial content draft only. Its embedded `Data/Seed/ev-la-mer-content.json` is generated from the frontend default: run `node scripts/generate-la-mer-seed.cjs` from the client after changing defaults, and use `--check` to verify parity. Existing prices, drafts and published revisions are never overwritten by initialization. Lesson rows keep their first names as lookup keys (`LaMerLessonLookupNames`, for example `ESL Classic｜综合英语`), so renaming a course on the page never inserts a duplicate row; the page maps database names to course ids with `laMerCourseId` / `laMerRoomId`.
