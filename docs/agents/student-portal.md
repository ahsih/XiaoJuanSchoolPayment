# Student applications, portal and notifications

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-11-26 to keep that file small. Record new decisions about this topic here; AGENTS.md holds only rules that apply to every task.

- `StudentApplication`: an internal registration dossier that supports a single student, a parent-child family, or a group of travellers. The primary contact owns the optional future Student-account link; additional members and independent multi-period course/accommodation plans are stored in structured versioned JSON while the legacy summary fields remain populated for list and portal compatibility. Enrollment date, overall enrollment status, and visa status are independent fields so staff can filter by signup month, not-yet-enrolled/enrolled/on-hold/cancelled records, and visa progress. Employees may create the dossier with only a primary name and school, add contact/study details later, and link the primary account automatically when a Student registers with the same email through a valid invitation. Employee changes are drafts with `Draft`/`PendingReview`/`Published` status; `PublishedSnapshotJson` preserves the last manager-approved student-visible version while a newer draft is edited or reviewed. One primary student can have multiple applications.
- `StudentApplicationDocument`: private application files such as quotes and offer letters. Files are stored outside `wwwroot` under `App_Data/student-documents`; additions and pending deletions follow the parent application's review workflow, and student downloads are limited to document IDs in the approved snapshot.
- `StudentPayment`: structured per-application payment records with amount, currency, payment type/date/method, receiving-account details, a private receipt file, submitter, and an independent `PendingReview`/`Confirmed`/`Returned` management-review workflow. Only confirmed student-visible payments appear in the student portal or count toward future receipt/payroll totals.
- Fees, consultant and requested documents (2026-11-25) are part of the application and its review workflow: `FeeItemsJson` (up to 20 items, each a name, amount, currency, optional due date and note), `RequestedDocumentsJson` (document types the student is asked to upload, from `STUDENT_SUBMISSION_TYPES`, kept identical in the server controller, `student-application.dto.ts` and the mini program's `utils/student-api.ts`) and `ConsultantName` (a name from `SIDA_PHILIPPINES_CONSULTANTS`; the portals show that consultant's avatar, WeChat QR and phone). Students see them only after publishing. The fee summary is computed on every read, per currency: due = sum of fee items, paid = sum of confirmed payments, remaining, and the next due date/amount (fee items covered in due-date order). School fees and local fees are entered as separate items, never added into one total.
- `StudentSubmission` (2026-11-25): files a Student uploads from the website `/student` or the mini program 学员中心 (passport page, photo, itinerary, visa, payment screenshot, insurance, other). PDF/JPG/PNG/WebP/DOCX up to 15 MB, checked by their first bytes, stored privately under `App_Data/student-documents/<application>/student-uploads/`, at most 60 per application and 20 waiting. They are not part of the published snapshot: each has its own `PendingReview`/`Accepted`/`Returned` status, set by any employee in the application's 资料与付款 dialog (returning one needs a reason). A student may delete an own upload until it is accepted. Each requested document shows the status of its latest upload (未上传／待确认／已收到／需重新上传).
- Student emails (2026-11-25, `Services/StudentNotifications.cs`): publishing an application emails the linked Student a short list of what changed (status, visa, new files, fees, requested documents, dates, consultant, notes; nothing when nothing visible changed), confirming a payment and returning an upload email the student, and a new upload emails `ContactForm:RecipientEmail` with a link to the admin dialog. Mail goes through an in-memory queue (`StudentEmailQueue`) and the `StudentEmailSender` background service with the `ContactForm` SMTP settings, so requests never wait for SMTP; unsent mail is lost on restart and is never logged with addresses or content.
- Student reminders (2026-11-25, `Services/StudentReminderService.cs`): an hourly background service, sending only 09:00–21:00 China time. An unpaid fee item is reminded 7 days before its due date and once more from 1 day before (`UnpaidFeeItems` covers fee items in due-date order, as the fee summary does; overdue items are not reminded again); a course start is reminded 14 days before and once more from 3 days before, only while requested documents are missing or fees are unpaid, and not for 已入学/已完成. Applications that are 已取消 or 保留/延期 (in the employees' version or the published one) get nothing. A student with an account is reminded by email (and WeChat, below) from the published snapshot and confirmed payments only. Every check also sends one staff email to `ContactForm:RecipientEmail` (`StaffFollowUps`) listing each application that entered a window, saying whether the student was reminded; a student with no account, or with nothing published yet, is listed from the employees' current version (`WorkingView`) so a consultant contacts them. Each reminder is recorded once in `StudentReminderLogs` (unique application + kind + key; the key holds the due or start date, the stage `first`/`final` and the fee item), so a changed date reminds again and nothing repeats after a restart. The admin list (`/admin/student-applications`) has matching 需要跟进 filters: 7天内要付款 (overdue included) and 14天内出发，资料或费用未齐; a card's follow-up note says when the student has no account. None of this can run on the user's PC (App Control), so check it on the live site after a deploy.
- WeChat reminders (2026-11-25): the mini program's 学员中心 asks for subscribe-message permission on a tap, then calls `POST /student-notifications/wechat/bind` with a `wx.login` code; the server exchanges it through `jscode2session` and stores only the openid (`SchoolUser.WeChatOpenId`; the session key is never kept). `GET /student-notifications/wechat` tells the page whether reminders are configured and which template ids to ask for; `DELETE /student-notifications/wechat` removes the openid (关闭微信提醒, and the mini program calls it when the student signs out). Every student email above (publish, payment confirmed, upload returned, fee due, departure) also queues a WeChat message for a bound student (`Services/WeChat/WeChatSubscribeMessages.cs`: `WeChatMessageQueue`, `WeChatMessageSender`, `stable_token` access token, page `pages/student/student`); a student who has no permission left (errcode 43101) simply gets the email. WeChat allows one message per permission, so the mini program tops the permission up silently on ordinary taps (opening a file, uploading) for templates the student set to 总是保持以上选择 (`wx.getSetting` with `withSubscriptions`); other students tap 再允许一次. Configuration is `WeChatMiniProgram` in appsettings: `AppId`, `AppSecret` (server environment only, `WeChatMiniProgram__AppSecret`, never committed), `MiniProgramState` (`formal`; use `trial` while testing with the 体验版, because the page must exist in the version the message opens) and `Templates.Progress`/`FeeDue`/`Departure`, each a `TemplateId` from the mini program console's 订阅消息 and `Fields` mapping the template's keys (for example `thing1`) to `{title}`, `{school}`, `{student}`, `{detail}`, `{state}` (a short all-Chinese word such as 待付款, for `phrase` fields), `{status}`, `{item}`, `{amount}` or `{date}`. `WeChatTemplateFormatter` trims every value to WeChat's rule for its key type (thing 20 characters, phrase 5 Chinese characters, name 10 Chinese characters or 20 letters, amount with a currency symbol, date and time in Chinese date form) and gives an empty value a neutral fallback, because one bad value makes WeChat reject the whole message (47003); a `time` field that rejects a plain date is retried with 00:00 added. WeChat answers are parsed from bytes, since `jscode2session` may answer JSON as text/plain. Only the kind, errcode and WeChat's own error text are logged, never the openid or message text. With no secret or no template the feature stays off and the mini program hides its card.
- Quote images to the student file (2026-11-25): on public school pages a signed-in Admin, Manager or Staff account sees 存到学员档案 beside the quote-image save button (`components/save-quote-to-student.component.ts`, added by `QuoteImageDownloadButtonComponent` only in the browser and only in download mode). It renders the same image and uploads it as a 学生报价单 document to the chosen application, so it joins the draft and reaches the student after publishing. It is not added under `/admin` or on the locked CIA page.

- `/student-applications`: Admin/Manager/Staff may create an internal dossier using only a primary name and school, choose single/family/group registration, add up to 19 companions, and record multiple course and accommodation periods per person. Each period stores its start date and week count; the end date is normalized to `start + weeks × 7 - 1 day`, and list/portal summaries derive from the structured plans. Creating a dossier does not create a login account; an existing Student account with the same primary email is linked, and future invited Student registration links an unclaimed dossier by normalized email. The employee screen is an internal-first student workbench with attention filters, completion summaries, sectioned drawer editing, private application documents, and structured payment submission. Staff/顾问 submit each payment with amount, currency, date, method and receipt; Admin/Manager independently confirms or returns it, and `/student-applications/payment-reviews` feeds the centralized payment-review queue. Application content still follows its separate required-summary review flow: Staff submit, Admin/Manager publish or return. `/student-applications/reviews` feeds the application review queue, while `/student-applications/me` returns the last published application snapshot plus only currently confirmed payments, the student's own uploads and the fee summary computed from the published fee items. `POST /student-applications/{id}/submissions` (Student only, `student-upload` rate limit of 20 per 10 minutes) uploads a file, `DELETE .../submissions/{submissionId}` removes an own not-yet-accepted upload, `GET .../submissions/{submissionId}/file` downloads it (employees or the owning student), and `.../accept` and `.../return` are for Admin/Manager/Staff. Private file downloads enforce employee access or matching Student ownership and approved visibility.
