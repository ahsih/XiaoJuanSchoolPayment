## Calculation choices

- Course price = six-person package; rooms are fixed supplements per 4 weeks (quad +80, triple +140, twin +240,
  single +500). This reproduces every cell of the price list; 1/2/3 weeks are 0.4/0.6/0.8 of the 4-week price.
- 思达启航 9折 on the package and the peak surcharge after the campaign discount; registration and peso fees are not discounted.
- Visa extensions: the school table counts from a 59-day entry (6,410 / 12,240 / 18,070 / 23,870 cumulative, i.e.
  6,410, 5,830, 5,830, 5,800). The same steps start at the first extension of either entry visa: a 30-day entry pays
  nothing for 4 weeks and 6,410 from the 5th week. Extensions past the table are estimated at 5,800 and say so; over 24 weeks adds the 5,000 exit clearance.
- Textbooks: 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000, then +1,000 every further 4 weeks (9–12 weeks 4,000).
