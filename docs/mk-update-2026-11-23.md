## Quote rules

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Course and room | Four-week dollar prices; 1/2/3 weeks at 40/65/85% (MK's ratios); four weeks and longer at the weekly average (user decision 2026-11-25) |
| Registration | 100 dollars once; waived for returning students |
| 2026 offer | Arrivals 2026-02-01 to 12-31: N weeks cost the N/2-week price for tuition and accommodation (MK's sheet); below 8 weeks an odd stay rounds the paid weeks up (5 weeks pay the 3-week price), from 8 weeks half weeks are proportional |
| 2027 offer | Arrivals in 2027 with course and dormitory: dormitory deduction 48/210.6/346.8/444/615/783/966 dollars for 1–7 weeks, 150 per week from 8 weeks (3-bed free) |
| TESOL | 350-dollar four-week add-on to an ESL or IELTS course; the WVSU SSP is an extra payment of unknown amount |
| Limits | Business and Working Holiday 18+ and at least 4 weeks; IELTS Guarantee at least 12 weeks |
| Sida discount | 10% on the full course and room price, applied first; MK's offers come off after it (user decision 2026-11-25) |

Local fees (pesos): SSP 7,500; SSP I-CARD 4,500; ACR I-CARD 4,300 with the first visa extension; extensions 5,040 / 6,420 / 4,330…, one per four weeks beyond the entry visa (a 30-day visa from week 5, a 59-day visa from week 9, starting at 6,420); management 1,000, water about 1,000 and electricity about 3,000 per started four accommodation weeks; ID 300. Long-term visas show SSP, SSP I-CARD, ACR and extensions at 0 with an adviser-confirmation note. Textbooks (about 500 per book), pickup (Iloilo 1,700 / Kalibo 4,000 for one person), the room deposit (5,000 plus a 150 key deposit) and the laundry coupon (150) are reference rows outside the total.

Checked examples with the Sida 10% (2027 arrival, ESL Basic): 3-bed 4 weeks 781 dollars; 3-bed 8 weeks 1,150; single 8 weeks 1,852; guardian + 4-bed 8 weeks 862. 2026 arrival, ESL Basic + 3-bed: 4 weeks 831.25, 5 weeks 1,056.25, 8 weeks 1,225.

## User decisions (2026-11-25)

- Follow MK's 2027 sheet for the 4-bed room: the full 150-dollar weekly deduction applies even where it exceeds the room fee.
- 思达启航 takes 10% off course and room.
- Extra-class prices are left off the page until their billing period is known.
- Four weeks and longer use the weekly average; shorter stays use MK's ratios.
