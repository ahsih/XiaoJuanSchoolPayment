## Quote rules

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Course and room | Four-week dollar prices; 1/2/3 weeks at 40/65/85%; four weeks and longer proportional |
| Registration | 100 dollars once; waived for returning students |
| 2026 offer | Arrivals 2026-02-01 to 12-31: N weeks cost the N/2-week price for tuition and accommodation (MK's sheet); odd stays under 8 weeks are blocked |
| 2027 offer | Arrivals in 2027 with course and dormitory: dormitory deduction 48/210.6/346.8/444/615/783/966 dollars for 1–7 weeks, 150 per week from 8 weeks (3-bed free) |
| TESOL | 350-dollar four-week add-on to an ESL or IELTS course; the WVSU SSP is an extra payment of unknown amount |
| Limits | Business and Working Holiday 18+ and at least 4 weeks; IELTS Guarantee at least 12 weeks |
| Sida discount | None (not in MK's files) |

Local fees (pesos): SSP 7,500; SSP I-CARD 4,500; ACR I-CARD 4,300 once the stay passes 59 days; extensions 5,040 / 6,420 / 4,330… (a 59-day visa starts at 6,420); management 1,000, water about 1,000 and electricity about 3,000 per started four accommodation weeks; ID 300. Long-term visas show SSP, SSP I-CARD, ACR and extensions at 0 with an adviser-confirmation note. Textbooks (about 500 per book), pickup (Iloilo 1,700 / Kalibo 4,000 for one person), the room deposit (5,000 plus a 150 key deposit) and the laundry coupon (150) are reference rows outside the total.

Checked examples (2027 arrival, ESL Basic): 3-bed 4 weeks 906 dollars; 3-bed 8 weeks 1,400; single 8 weeks 2,180. 2026 arrival, ESL Basic + 3-bed 4 weeks: 912.5 (MK's sheet).

## Open points for the user

- MK's 2027 sheet applies the full 150-dollar weekly deduction to the 4-bed room too, so from 8 weeks it exceeds the 130-dollar weekly room fee and lowers tuition (guardian + 4-bed 8 weeks = 1,080). The page follows MK's sheet.
- MK's files give no Sida discount, so none is applied.
- The extra-class prices (ESL one-to-one 150 dollars, etc.) do not state their billing period; the page says to confirm it with the school.
- MK is not in the employee content editors yet, and the database has no MK course or room rows; the page uses compiled prices.
