## Decisions and interpretations

- Prices by registration date (the school's 2026 list applies "to students registered after 2025-03-06"; the 2027 transition box keeps current terms until 2026-12-31): registrations until 2026-12-31 pay the 2026 price for the raised items (EOP Mansion Master Single 1,100; City Native ESL 900, Unlimited ESL 900, Semi Master Single 1,050, Semi Regular Single 900); from 2027-01-01 the 2027 list. Stored as `price2026` on those rows; the quote asks the registration date (default today). **Confirm with the school** if a December 2026 registration for a 2027 arrival is really charged the 2026 price.
- Off-season: 2026 periods for all campuses (as before); 2027 periods (02/07–06/13, 09/05–12/26) EOP and City only. Arrival day decides, whole stay 10% on course and room; long-stay tiers by course weeks after it.
- Peak: rate from `association-peak-surcharge.ts` ('BECI': 40 in 2026, 60 in 2027); 2027 range ends 08/21 per BECI (A&J uses 08/22). Early bird is a negative line equal to the 2027 peak part.
- Registration: 100 dollars, waived for every 思达启航 student (earlier decision, kept).
- Local fees (2027 PDF): SSP 7,800, E-Card 4,500, ACR 4,000 only for stays over 59 days (the school's rule, not the general "first extension" rule), visa extension totals by months of stay and entry visa (table in the content document), ID 200, textbooks per started 4 weeks per course (Lite 1,000, Speed 1,500, others 2,000; Junior IELTS and IELTS Guarantee take 2,000 from the EOP and Sparta orientation guides), utilities 750 and maintenance 250 per week (orientation guides), laundry 1,500 per started 4 weeks, BESA pickup 3,000 (2027 PDF; the 2026 City guide said Clark 2,500), deposit 3,000 outside totals, Sparta group-to-one-to-one upgrade 500 a week when chosen. Stays are capped at 24 weeks (one SSP, visa table to 6 months).
- City pair prices (2027): Studio Single shared by two 800 each, Studio Double together 750 each; need two students in a group quote.
- Ages: EOP/City 15+ (14 and under with a guardian, text only); EOP 40+ single rooms only (blocked otherwise); Sparta 18–40 (blocked otherwise).
- Not published: commission line of the peak notice, the school's pre-departure cancellation penalties, campus addresses (Sparta film's opening address card cut), the nationality-ratio images.
- Open: IELTS Guarantee daily classes in 2027 ("—" in the 2027 file, shown as 待学校确认 with the 2026 line); guaranteed score and entry level not stated by the school; photos of Mansion Regular Single/Triple/Quad and Sparta Quad.
