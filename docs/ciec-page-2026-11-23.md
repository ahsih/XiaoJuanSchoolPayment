The user supplied the CIEC school pack `2026-11-23.zip`, which contained:

- the 2027 Chinese brochure;
- the 2027 tuition sheet `v.270101`;
- the 2027 low-season offer;

The user asked for the CIEC page to be updated from this pack. This replaces the 2026-10-08 calculator described in `ciec-quote-update-2026-10-08.md`.

## What changed

- `/philippines-study/cebu/ciec` now renders `CiecSchoolPageComponent` on the shared template (`school-page/`), in CIA's compact layout with `layoutVersion: 2` quote and comparison images.
- Removed:
  - `ciec-quote.ts`;
  - `ciec-quote.spec.ts`;
  - `ciec-quote-calculator.component.*`;
  - `ciec-school.component.*`;
  - `public/assets/ciec/quote-banner.svg`.
- New modules, all pure data and calculation:
  - `ciec-content-config.ts`: CIEC's content document, with courses, rooms, local fees and promotions.
  - `ciec-page-content.ts`: all page text and data.
  - `ciec-page-profile.ts`: fills the template.
  - `ciec-page-quote.ts`: `CiecPageStudent`, `CiecQuote` and `CiecPlanComparison`.
- The template gained one optional flag, `PhPageStudent.hideAgeGroup`. CIEC sets it to hide the age select. CIA, EV, CPI, CPILS and CELLA do not set it, so their pages are unchanged.
- Prices come only from the content document. `applyPricingData` is a no-op, because the database has only the regional starting-price row `菲律宾宿务CIEC`, which is still used to resolve the school.
- `cebu-study.component.ts` now uses the new campus photo for the CIEC card.

## Prices and rules (from the 2027 tuition sheet and low-season offer)

### Prices

- 2027 prices are the same as 2026. All prices are per 4 weeks.
  - Courses (dollars):

    | Course | Price |
    | --- | ---: |
    | Kindergarten | 1,050 |
    | Junior academic | 1,100 |
    | Junior intensive | 1,400 |
    | Junior IELTS/TOEFL | 1,400 |
    | School preparation | 1,400 |
    | Overseas school | 1,100 |
    | CIDEC | 1,350 |
    | Parent ESL | 700 |
    | Parent Business/IELTS | 800 |

  - Rooms (dollars): double 930, triple 850, four-person triple 770.
- Registration is 100 dollars per person; returning students pay none.
- Stays of 2 weeks cost 65% of the 4-week price and 3 weeks 85%. One-week stays are not offered.

### Who studies what

- Every person in a quote is one of three roles: a child with a parent, a parent (家长／监护人), or a child without a parent. The child's age is entered separately.
- Children under 4 take 不上课.
  - Up to 24 months: no room and board.
  - 25–47 months: half room and board.
  - Neither counts as a room occupant, but management and water are charged.
- Kindergarten is for ages 4–6 and needs a parent. Junior courses start at age 6.
- Parents must take a parent course, except when:
  - the stay is over 12 weeks;
  - a child under 4 comes along; or
  - a low-season family offer applies.
- A child without a parent pays a guardian fee of 300 dollars per 4 weeks.
- CIDEC adds a one-time grade tuition of 3,000 dollars, which is inside the Sida 95%. It needs at least 4 weeks and has its own 15,000-peso SSP + E-CARD.
- Optional Cebu pickup is 20 dollars per person.

### Low-season offers

The low-season windows are:

- 2026/08/31–2027/01/01
- 2027/03/01–05/21
- 2027/08/30–12/31

A week qualifies when its study days, Monday to Friday, fall inside a window, and the student must register for at least 4 weeks. The offers:

- **One parent and one child in a double room:** the parent's room costs 300 dollars (2026) or 400 dollars (2027) per 4 weeks.
- **One parent and two children in a triple room:** the parent's room is free.
- **Parent courses:** 400 dollars (ESL) or 500 dollars (Business/IELTS) per 4 weeks.
- **Children aged 9 or over without a parent:** no guardian fee.
- **Primary-school children in the 2026 window:** a non-cash craft class.
- **Room upgrade (both notices, when a room is free):** a 2-person family may live in a triple room. Each person pays 200 dollars per 4 weeks more than in the double room: the child 1,130 dollars and the parent the low-season price + 200. The quote offers this as the room 三人间（2人家庭升级）, which needs both family members in it, only in low-season weeks.

The Sida 95% applies to tuition, room and CIDEC tuition after these offers.

### Other price rules

- The four-person triple price needs exactly four family members in the room, not counting infants.
- There is no peak surcharge. The 500-dollar peak-month family deposit is explained on the page only.

### Local fees (2027 sheet)

- SSP is 8,000 pesos and E-CARD 4,500, for everyone who studies.
- I-CARD is 4,000 pesos for stays over 59 days.
- Visa extensions cost, in order: 4,800 / 5,800 / 5,300 / 5,500 / 5,500 / 4,000 pesos.
  - A 30-day entry visa first extends 29 days, to day 59.
  - A 59-day entry visa starts from the second step.
- Management is 2,400 pesos and water 1,200 pesos, per person per 4 weeks.
- Group or kindergarten copies cost 1,000 pesos per 4 course weeks.
- ID is 200 pesos.
- These are reference rows, not in the total: books, electricity (23 pesos/kWh), deposit, kindergarten kit, sending and extra nights.
- A long-term visa sets the visa rows to zero and adds the adviser-confirmation note.

## Page content

- Photos are the brochure's own, at native size in `public/assets/ciec/`, with no upscaling:
  - 29 photos of the campus, classrooms, rooms, canteen and facilities;
  - the hero and quote image use `campus-entrance.webp`.
- Each room card links to its photos through `rateIds`, because CIEC rooms have no booking codes.
- 方案对比 prices one person, so it leaves out the family-only rooms, the four-person triple and the 2-person upgrade (`onePersonRooms` in `ciec-page-quote.ts`).
- The daily timetable has a junior column and a kindergarten column, taken from the brochure examples.
- The 2027 holiday table lists weekday holidays only, from the school poster.
  - EDSA (2/25) is a working day.
  - The two Eid holidays are not yet proclaimed; the footnote says so.
- The practical guide and dorm rules come from 入学说明: first day, meals, services, check-out, safety and warnings.
- Test results (user decision 2026-11-24): six real CIEC test reports, three with skill levels (L1–L6) and three with 9-point band scores, in `public/assets/ciec/test-results/`. Student names and the school's address and phone are pixelated and empty remark boxes cut off. They are internal school tests, so the template's results block uses the new optional `summary` and `kind` fields instead of its 官方雅思 wording (CIA renders unchanged).
- Refund (user decision 2026-11-24): the folded refund section follows the new-student guide (before arrival, after arrival, special cases, local fees) and adds 思达启航's own rule: the 2,000-yuan deposit paid when registering is never refunded (the start date may change if asked at least 4 weeks before entry, or the deposit is kept for one year), and rescheduling less than 4 weeks before arrival may cost a penalty.
- Left out:
  - **Videos:** CIEC has none yet.
  - **The two long teacher reports:** whole pages of text about one student.
  - **Brochure wording such as 成功率100% and 最著名:** the brand fact rules forbid it.
  - **School contacts, website and address:** never published on school pages.
