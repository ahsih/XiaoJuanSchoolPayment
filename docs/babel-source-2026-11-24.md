## Photos

`public/assets/babel/` holds 10 WebP photos (at most 1400 px) of the school and adult classes from the school's website gallery and the adult flyer. The 12 camp photos were removed with the camp section. Each was checked: only Babel's own logo, no other company and no contact details. The accommodation photos in the price list are too small (300–500 px) and are not used. The school's SharePoint marketing folder needs a Microsoft sign-in, so no videos are on the page yet.

- Testimonials with student names; reviews are shown by country and programme only.
- The junior summer camps, the school-group programme and the DCU parent-child programme (user decision 2026-11-24), with their photos and camp reviews.
- The school's struck-through "original" 25-week prices (3,600 / 2,895 / 4,450 euros).
