# CIP page on the shared template (2026-11-24)

`/philippines-study/clark/cip-english-kepos` now renders `CipSchoolPageComponent` on the shared Philippine school
template in CIA's compact layout, with `layoutVersion: 2` quote and comparison images. It replaces the standalone
page of 2026-09-20 (`cip-school.component.*`, `cip-pricing.ts`, `cip-student-quote.ts`, `cip-content.ts`,
`CipQuoteCalculatorComponent` and `scripts/verify-cip-page.cjs` were removed). The two 2026-09-20 documents
(`cip-source-audit-2026-09-20.md`, `cip-implementation-2026-09-20.md`) describe that old renminbi page and are
kept only as history.

## Sources

The school's own pack, received 2026-11-24 through two shared folders. The school asked that the links are never
passed to students, so they are not written anywhere in this repository, on the page or in commits. Files used:

- June 2026 dollar price list (courses, campus rooms and Clover Hotel rooms, 1–24 weeks).
- School message: students entering from 2027 are quoted in dollars; no peak surcharge in June–August; student
  score reports are not public unless the student agrees.
- 2026–2027 off-season poster (50 dollars per full 4 study weeks, gifts, free Clark pickup).
- June 2026 dormitory local-fee sheet.
- 2026 course summary, school rules, 2025–2026 student handbook and the room introduction.
- 2027 holiday poster.
- Campus, classroom, dining, room and hotel photos and eight school videos.

Not published: the school's contacts, website, social accounts, address, the documents themselves, the agency
discount sheet, score reports and the price list header (it names the school's discount group).

## Quote rules

- Dollars only, for arrivals from 2027-01-03. Earlier arrivals are blocked with a note to ask a consultant
  (the school quotes those in renminbi).
- Registration 100 dollars per student; returning students free.
- 1/2/3 weeks are 40/65/85% of the 4-week price; from 4 weeks proportional. The price list's combined totals
  equal course + room, checked for several rows.
- Long stay: 16/20/24 course weeks take 50/100/150 dollars off (the price list's totals).
- 2027 off-season: 50 dollars per full 4 study weeks outside June–August through 2027-12-31; June–August
  weeks never count. The non-cash gift (one of three upgrades) shows as a 淡季福利 row; free Clark pickup in the
  off-season.
- 思达启航95折 last: 5% of tuition and accommodation less the school offers (see School answers).
- No peak surcharge.
- Course lengths: Speak Up 1–2 weeks, IELTS Basic 4 weeks, IELTS Guarantee 8 or 12 weeks, several courses at
  most 12 or 16 weeks (`CIP_COURSE_RULES`).
- Full-Sparta courses (Intensive ESL and IELTS) cannot stay at the hotel.
- Ages: adults take any adult course; 7–11 Primary English; 12–15 Junior ESL or Junior Native; 16–17 only
  full-Sparta courses. Minors cannot stay alone in the hotel king room.
- Local fees (pesos): visa extensions 5,640 / 7,420 / 4,940 / 5,450 / 5,950 (30-day entry from week 5; 59-day
  entry starts at 7,420 from week 9), ACR I-CARD 4,000 with the first extension, SSP 6,800, SSP I-CARD 4,000,
  electricity 600 a week (campus dormitory only), facility fee 500 a week, ID 250, textbooks about 2,000 per
  4 course weeks. Long-term visas zero the visa rows with the adviser-confirmation note.
- References outside the total: dormitory deposit by length (none at the hotel), Clark/Manila pickup, laundry,
  extra nights 3,000 a night.

## School answers (2026-11-24)

The school answered the open price questions on 2026-11-24: prices follow the fee box at the top right of each
price-list page, and agencies may give students at most 5%.

- IELTS Guarantee: the fee box gives 1,320 dollars per 4 weeks for the 8-week course and 1,200 for the 12-week
  course, so 12 weeks cost 3,600 (the content's `feeByWeeks`), although the list's 12-week totals use 1,320. The
  course list shows 1,200 美元/4周起.
- Hotel king room, 2 people: 1,326 per person, the fee box price (the course totals use 1,325).
- Power ESL at the hotel: 1,020 + room, the fee box price (the list's hotel rows use 720, the Light ESL price).
- 思达启航95折 (rule `cip-sida-95`): 5% of tuition and accommodation, dormitory and hotel alike, after the school's
  cash offers (off-season and long stay), as the school's 2026 tuition discount policy orders it (总学费 without
  registration, school offers first); registration and local fees are not discounted. The amount is rounded down
  to the cent so it never goes past 5%. The user chose tuition + accommodation as the base (2026-11-24), because the
  policy's 总学费 excludes only registration. `cloneCipContentConfig` adds the rule to a saved document that lacks it.
  The cap is not published.
- The December 2026 offer (4 weeks for the price of 3) is shown as an offer card only, because 2026 arrivals are
  quoted in renminbi.
