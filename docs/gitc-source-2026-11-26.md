## Prices (US dollars per 4 weeks)

Price period: the 2027 list applies to courses starting on or after Monday 2027-02-15 (arrival Sunday 2027-02-14), user decision 2026-11-26 (“by arrival date”). Earlier courses pay the 2026 list for the whole stay.

Tuition, both campuses: Basic 1:1(3)+G(2) 700 / 700; Regular 1:1(4)+G(3) 800 / 850; Power Speaking 1:1(7) 900 / 1,000; Pre-IELTS and Pre-TOEIC 1:1(3)+G(4) 800 / 1,200; IELTS and TOEIC Guarantee 900 / 1,300 (2026: 1:1(3)+G(4); 2027: 1:1(4)+G(4)). Iloilo Junior 1:1(4)+G(2) 800 on the 2026 list only. (2026 / 2027.)

Iloilo rooms (accommodation + meals, per person): One Spatial Single A 610 / 750; Avida single 650 / 800; SMDC single 650 (2026 “Avida·SMDC”) / about 900–1,500 “after a check”; Mega World single 750 / about 900–1,500; One Spatial Single B (two-bedroom, one room each) 510 / 600; twin (family or friends) One Spatial 430 / 530, Avida 450 / 580, SMDC 450 / about 700–900, Mega World 500 / about 700–900; family triple (2026 list only) One Spatial 420, Avida and SMDC 560, Mega World 685. 2027 also lists Avida/SMDC/Mega World two-bedroom single rooms at about 800–1,300 after a check.

Cebu rooms (per person, 3 meals): on-campus capsule single Regular Size 300 / 450, Big Size 320 / 550; off-campus condo single 850 / 980, twin 600 / 750 (condo includes the school bus).

Every 2027 package price in the brochure equals tuition + room, checked for every row. Registration 100; extra one-to-one 150 per class per 4 weeks; 1/2/3 weeks 40/75/90% of 4 weeks.

User decisions 2026-11-26: SMDC and Mega World 2027 prices show as 待学校确认 and are not quoted. Local fees come from the 2026-09-16 sheet, not from the brochure's fee table.
