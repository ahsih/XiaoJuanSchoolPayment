## Quote rules

- Prices are per 4 weeks: 2027 prices equal the 2026 list. Registration is 100 dollars once per student, and returning students waive it. One to three weeks cost 35/60/80% of four weeks; longer stays are proportional.
- 2027 off-season offer, for stays from Sunday 2027/02/28 with at least 2 weeks inside 2027/02/28–06/26 (only the weeks inside the window count):
  - Courses: 50 dollars per course week for ESL 4–6, Native Mix, the exam and business courses, and the junior courses. ESL 3 (parents) gets 25 dollars per week.
  - Rooms: 50 dollars per week for WE Residence and for the dormitory single and twin rooms. The PDF's "FAMILY" rooms are read as both the Big Size Room and the Green House; this still needs school confirmation.
  - A free native option group class every day. This is shown as a non-cash 赠 line.
  - Nov–Dec 2026: the school's registration info says bookings come "with promotion now", but no supplied file gives the terms, so no Nov–Dec 2026 offer is calculated until the user sends them.
- 思达启航折扣 is taken from the full course plus room price, before the school offers. WE's files set no order, so the user's rule (2026-11-24) applies: use the order that is cheaper for the student, within the agent-discount rate the school allows. Four weeks of ESL 4 in a dorm twin room from 2027-03-07 cost 1,140 dollars (100 + 1,600 − 160 − 400). The rates are:
  - 10% (9折) normally.
  - 5% (95折) when a WE Residence stay touches the busy season, the school's maximum in the Residence notice.
  - None for a parent who stays without classes.
- Busy season is December–February and June–August. A study week is busy when its Monday or Friday falls in one of those months.
  - There is no surcharge.
  - The 300-dollar deposit is paid with registration and counts toward the full fee (user, 2026-11-24). It is not an extra charge and is not added to the total; the orange note under the total says when to pay it.
  - WE Residence needs at least 4 weeks in the busy season and 2 weeks otherwise. The dormitory needs 2 weeks in the busy season.
- Course and room combinations:
  - A WE Residence parent may stay without classes: the `guardian-no-class` course at 0 dollars, with no SSP. This is blocked with dormitory rooms.
  - ESL 3, the junior courses and kindergarten cannot use the dormitory single room.
  - Child courses need the minor age group, and parent courses need the adult group.
- Local fees, in pesos, are kept separate from the school total:
  - SSP 7,800 and SSP E-CARD 4,000.
  - ACR I-CARD 4,000 when the stay is over 59 days.
  - Dormitory service 500 per week; WE Residence service 1,000 per week.
  - Visa extensions: a 30-day entry pays 4,800 / 6,000 / 4,500, then 4,500 each; a 59-day entry starts at 6,000.
  - Student ID 300.
  - Reference rows outside the total: textbooks, room electricity (22 pesos/kWh), Clark and Manila pickup, the dormitory deposit and the WE Residence deposit.

## Content decisions

- The page never shows:
  - the school's phone, email, website, social accounts, address or map link;
  - agent commission or source citations;
  - the Google Maps reviews.
- The brochure's superlatives (0% crime, safest school) and its flight-route wording are not used.
- 2027 holidays come from the school's calendar.
  - The June 15 holiday is shown as moved to Friday 06/18.
  - Rizal Day and New Year's Eve, typed "-26" in the school's list, are treated as 2027 dates (their weekdays match).
- There is no licence strip, because the permits were not supplied, and no refund section.
- These videos were not used:
  - first day, graduation camp, the Vietnam junior camp and "say it in your language";
  - the Japanese interviews, the hotel family rooms and the new-residence vlog;
  - the 2024 slideshow and the outside clips.
- Photo labels follow what each photo shows. For example, the "playground" photo shows children playing football on the lawn and is labelled 草坪球场.
